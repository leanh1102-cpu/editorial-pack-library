# Terms

Vietnamese-first terminology is preferred. Add an international term on first use when materially useful.

## Foundation terms
- **dữ kiện quan sát được / observable feature** — wording, action, syntax, recurrence, object, punctuation, or contextual fact directly supplied by the text.
- **suy luận / inference** — conclusion drawn from evidence; not identical to textual fact.
- **phát ngôn / utterance** — concrete communicative event situated in speaker, addressee, time, place, and context.
- **nghĩa câu / sentence meaning** — linguistic meaning attributable to the sentence form apart from a specific situated use.
- **lực ngôn trung / illocutionary force** — communicative act/function performed by an utterance under relevant conditions.
- **cấu trúc thông tin / information structure** — organization of information status/focus that can interact with surface form.
- **tiêu điểm / focus** — information made especially relevant/contrasted in a communicative context.
- **tỉnh lược / ellipsis** — omitted material recoverable only when context/information structure licenses recovery.
- **đánh dấu / markedness** — departure from a neutral/default pattern that may be functional rather than erroneous.
- **tiền cảnh hóa / foregrounding** — increased textual prominence produced by deviation from a relevant expectation or by patterning/parallelism; prominence alone does not prove quality or correctness.
- **chuẩn nội tại / internal norm** — a locally established textual pattern used as the relevant background for judging markedness or deviation inside that text.
- **nhịp / rhythm** — patterned timing/pressure produced by sentence length, pause, fracture, repetition, syntax, and arrangement.
- **giọng / voice** — patterned linguistic behavior associated with narrator/speaker plus current speech condition.
- **register / phong cách-ngữ vực** — language selection shaped by role, relation, setting, purpose, institution, period, and social conditions; not reducible to vocabulary density.
- **hàm ý / implicature** — inferred meaning beyond direct expression, recoverable from context/reasoning but not automatically objective fact.
- **mạch lạc / coherence** — interpretable semantic/logical connectedness across units; not equivalent to explicit transition density.
- **liên kết / cohesion** — formal/reference/lexical resources that connect textual units; one contributor to coherence, not its synonym.
- **hình tượng / image** — textually constructed perceptual/associative configuration, not merely decorative wording.
- **neo cụ thể / concrete grounding** — direct scene/body/object/contact signal used when it materially improves precision/function.
- **trung gian cảm giác / sensory mediation** — redundant cognition/nominalization/causal packaging between stimulus and sensation when it demonstrably weakens the sentence.
- **đọc gần / close reading** — evidence-led examination of textual signals, relations, form, and effects before interpretation.
- **chuyển giao thủ pháp / craft transfer** — preserving a mechanism/effect under new constraints while changing surface diction, imagery, and syntax.
- **rủi ro bắt chước / imitation risk** — danger that a transfer copies signature surface style instead of abstracting mechanism.
- **giả cổ cơ học / pseudo-archaic inflation** — surface archaizing produced by unsupported lexical substitution, register mixture, or similar signals without sufficient historical evidence; a diagnostic warning, not an authenticity verdict.

## Intermediate terms
- **khung truy cập / access frame** — locally licensed range of perception, thought, knowledge, or reporting available to narrator/focalizer.
- **điểm nhìn / focalization-point of view** — organization of narrative access and perception; not reducible to pronoun choice.
- **hồi cố / analepsis** — marked movement to earlier narrative material.
- **dự thuật / prolepsis** — marked movement toward later narrative material.
- **thời lượng kể / narrative duration** — relation between story-time and discourse space; may compress, scene, pause, or stretch.
- **tần suất kể / narrative frequency** — relation between repeated events and the number/form of narrative tellings.
- **nợ nhân quả / causal debt** — project operational term for an established dependency/consequence that remains active across later scenes until accounted for.
- **hành động xã hội của lời thoại / dialogue as social action** — what an utterance does in relation and context beyond literal propositional content.
- **độc thoại nội tâm / interior monologue** — representation of character thought/interiority; surface typography varies.
- **hòa giọng người kể–nhân vật / narrator-character blending** — narration carrying character-colored consciousness without necessarily becoming quoted direct thought.
- **logic hành vi / behavioral logic** — longitudinal relation among baseline, state, pressure, relation, purpose, preparation, action, and aftermath.
- **miêu tả chọn lọc / selective description** — detail selection shaped by access, action, pressure, relation, clue, mood, characterization, continuity, or payoff.
- **mô-típ / motif** — recurrent textual element whose recurrence is evidence of pattern but not automatic proof of one fixed symbolic meaning.
- **tái diễn giải công bằng / fair reinterpretation** — internal operational term for a later reveal that changes interpretation while preserving earlier facts and a reconstructable reread path.
- **tiết lộ phân cấp / graded revelation** — internal operational term for staged increases in certainty without premature confirmation.

## Advanced term set
Validated Advanced distinctions use these terms operationally: **reliability signal; narrator framing; dialogism; polyphony; reaccenting; fragmentation; metafiction; intertextual relation; allusion confidence; motif network; symbol recontextualization; hybridity; textual provenance; edition status; translation criterion; productive opacity; reread path**.

Definitions must preserve source/evidence scope. Do not import broad historical/form claims into A6/A7 without source support.

## Writer Self Review terms
- **material failure** — a demonstrated problem in meaning, function, evidence, continuity, access, causality, orientation, voice/register, rhythm, or intended reader effect.
- **detection** — noticing a problem signal without yet identifying its mechanism.
- **diagnosis** — identifying the causal/textual mechanism that produces the failure.
- **revision strategy** — an intervention selected because it addresses the diagnosed mechanism.
- **revision scale** — the smallest textual/system level that contains the actual failure.
- **author-memory contamination** — using information remembered by the writer but absent from the current manuscript as if readers possess it.
- **must-preserve set** — features/effects that a revision is not allowed to damage.
- **targeted pass** — a review pass organized around one primary diagnostic question.
- **regression** — a new material failure introduced by a revision that improves another dimension.
- **saturation stop** — stop condition when no material failure remains and proposed changes are preference-only.
- **best-defensible release** — release after the pass cap using the strongest non-regressed version available, with unresolved debt captured rather than hidden.
- **learning capture** — record of a narrow failure mechanism, scope, counterexample, regression risk, and future test; not a universal style law.

## Global scope lock
These are operational/curriculum definitions. They do not replace specialist historical linguistics, poetics, rhetoric, reference grammar, narratology, textual criticism, or translation-studies definitions.
