# KX7 — Point of View and Access

**Status:** `CANDIDATE-STABLE / VALIDATED`

## CONCEPT
Point of view is the organization of narrative information through narrator position, focal/access frame, perceptual and cognitive availability, and shifts in who can know or perceive what. A POV diagnosis must distinguish legitimate information control from accidental leakage of author-only knowledge.

Core route:

`NARRATOR CONTRACT → ACCESS FRAME → AVAILABLE INFORMATION → WITHHOLDING/SHIFT → LEAKAGE? → LOCAL ACTION`

## DEFINITION
- **Narrator:** the narrating instance or voice presenting the story.
- **Focal/access frame:** the perspective-relative restriction or selection through which information is made available at a given moment.
- **Perceptual availability:** what can be seen, heard, touched, or otherwise sensed from the active frame.
- **Cognitive availability:** what the active frame can know, remember, infer, believe, or misremember.
- **Strategic withholding:** information intentionally not disclosed even though a broader narrator may possess it.
- **POV leakage:** information presented as available inside a restricted frame even though the focal character has no established route to it.
- **Marked shift:** a change in access frame or narrative distance that is signaled or functionally established rather than accidental.

## SCOPE
Use this module when reviewing close third person, first person, shifting focalization, narrator access, free-indirect blending, strategic concealment, or suspected POV inconsistency.

Do not use it to:
- force every scene into one focalizer;
- equate narrator and focal character automatically;
- treat every contradiction as deception or unreliable narration;
- remove broader narrator knowledge when the narrative contract has established it;
- repair one local access breach by changing the entire book's POV.

## MECHANISM
### 1. Establish the narrative contract
Ask what kind of access the surrounding text has established: strict first-person limitation, close third, flexible third, overt narrator commentary, rotating focalization, or another pattern.

### 2. Identify the active access frame
Track whose perception, knowledge, memory, or inference structures the current passage.

### 3. Separate perception from knowledge
Seeing an envelope does not grant access to its contents. Knowing a fact may come from memory, prior dialogue, document access, or inference, but the channel must be textually supported when it matters.

### 4. Distinguish withholding from leakage
- broader narrator knows but withholds → potentially legitimate information strategy;
- restricted focal frame states inaccessible content with no channel → leakage candidate.

### 5. Allow functional shifts
A narrative may move among access frames. Judge whether the shift is signaled, recoverable, and functionally integrated rather than treating multiplicity itself as error.

### 6. Repair at the scale of the defect
If one sentence violates an otherwise coherent close-third frame, remove/reframe only the inaccessible assertion when possible. Do not globalize the repair.

## TEXTUAL SIGNALS
- direct statements of another character's unobserved thought inside a restricted frame;
- precise contents of unseen documents;
- sudden knowledge of offstage events without established narrator access;
- shifts in deictic language, sensory detail, interiority, or evaluative diction;
- free-indirect language blending narrator and character idiom;
- rotating scene focalizers;
- narrator commentary exceeding character knowledge;
- deliberate omission followed by later reinterpretation.

## EFFECTS
POV/access choices can create:
- intimacy or distance;
- uncertainty and suspense;
- dramatic irony;
- restricted inference;
- strategic concealment;
- contrast among characters' knowledge;
- reread value;
- epistemic instability.

These effects depend on an intelligible narrative contract; confusion caused by accidental access leakage is not automatically productive mystery.

## CONDITIONS
Preserve a POV choice when:
- access is compatible with the established narrator contract;
- a shift is signaled or structurally recoverable;
- broader narrator access has been established;
- withholding preserves fair inference without fabricating mandatory facts;
- contradiction belongs to belief, memory, or unreliability rather than continuity error.

Repair when:
- a restricted focal character receives author-only knowledge with no channel;
- the text changes access frame accidentally and causes material misorientation;
- narration collapses claim/belief into story fact unintentionally;
- a local leak breaks a clue, legal fact, mystery, or causal dependency.

## COUNTEREXAMPLES
- In close third through Mai, `Trong túi áo Lan, lá thư của Hòa ghi đúng ba cái tên` is a local access breach if Mai has never seen the letter.
- A narrator with established broader access can state information no focal character currently knows without violating POV.
- A novel can rotate focalization among characters; multiplicity is not inconsistency by itself.
- A situated narrator may state something false because of self-deception; contradiction with later evidence does not automatically mean authorial error.
- Strategic withholding can be fair when true clues remain available and later revelation reinterprets rather than invents required facts.

## COMMON ERRORS
- narrator = focalizer by default;
- close third = no narrator flexibility ever;
- every viewpoint shift is head-hopping;
- every contradiction proves an unreliable narrator;
- giving intelligent characters information they could not know;
- fixing one local access problem by rewriting the scene architecture;
- revealing withheld information merely to protect readers from uncertainty;
- treating author outline knowledge as character evidence.

## HISTORICAL VARIATION
`SOURCE-GATED`.

Narrative conventions and narrator-reader relations vary by period and tradition. Do not impose modern close-third expectations on older narrative forms without relevant source support.

## FORM VARIATION
Narrative access behaves differently across first-person memoir, epistolary fiction, omniscient narration, oral-style storytelling, documentary fiction, metafiction, and other forms. Form-specific rules remain source-gated where the current package lacks authoritative coverage.

## CLOSE READING METHOD
Build an access map:

`WHO NARRATES → ACTIVE FRAME → WHAT CAN BE PERCEIVED → WHAT CAN BE KNOWN → WHAT IS ONLY INFERRED/BELIEVED → WHAT IS WITHHELD → WHAT CHANGES`

For each suspicious sentence, ask whether the information channel is established before labeling it a violation.

## CRAFT TRANSFER
Transfer access mechanics rather than viewpoint labels.

Examples:
- recreate restricted knowledge using a different character and sensory channel;
- recreate dramatic irony by giving the reader a fact through a different narrator frame while preserving character ignorance;
- recreate a marked shift using a different scene boundary and different linguistic signals.

## DRILLS
1. Mark every proposition in a paragraph as `PERCEIVED / KNOWN / REMEMBERED / INFERRED / UNAVAILABLE`.
2. Repair one local POV leak without changing scene architecture.
3. Compare strategic withholding with missing fair-play evidence.
4. Diagnose whether a viewpoint shift is signaled and functional or accidental.
5. Separate narrator assertion, character belief, and story fact in an unreliable narration example.

## AI OPERATIONAL RULES
- Distinguish narrator from focal/access frame.
- Track perceptual and cognitive availability separately.
- Intelligence does not grant hidden information.
- Do not label contradiction alone as deception or unreliability.
- Legitimate viewpoint shifts may be preserved when signaled/recoverable and functional.
- Strategic withholding is not a POV error when the narrative contract supports it and fair evidence remains.
- Repair local leakage locally when possible; do not homogenize POV globally.
- Never promote deleted outline/author memory into character or reader evidence.

## SELF-CHECK
- What narrator contract does the surrounding text establish?
- Whose access frame is active here?
- Can this information be perceived, known, remembered, or reasonably inferred by that frame?
- Did I confuse narrator knowledge with character knowledge?
- Is the suspicious difference a deliberate shift, withholding, or actual leakage?
- Did I call contradiction unreliability without enough evidence?
- Can the repair stay local?
