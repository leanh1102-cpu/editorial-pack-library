# KX1 — Close Reading

**Status:** `CANDIDATE-STABLE / VALIDATED / COMPOSITE EVIDENCE`

## CONCEPT
Close reading is evidence-led analysis of how specific textual choices produce local and larger effects. In this skill it is a route from observable language to bounded interpretation and then, when useful, to craft transfer.

Primary route:

`OBSERVATION → INFERENCE → INTERPRETATION → ALTERNATIVE READING → CRAFT TRANSFER`

Expanded lens route inherited from Foundation F8:

`chữ → cú pháp → nhịp → hình thức → điểm nhìn/giọng → cấu trúc → hiệu ứng → diễn giải → craft transfer`

The expanded route is a menu, not a mandatory checklist. A passage need not activate every lens.

## DEFINITION
- **Observation:** what the text demonstrably contains: wording, syntax, repetition, punctuation, image, reference, ordering, viewpoint access, juxtaposition, omission, or other recoverable feature.
- **Inference:** a conclusion reasonably drawn from observations but not itself explicitly stated.
- **Interpretation:** an account of how multiple observations/inferences organize meaning or effect.
- **Alternative reading:** another interpretation compatible with materially relevant evidence.
- **Craft transfer:** extracting a mechanism that can be realized with different surface material rather than copying diction, objects, syntax, or plot furniture.

## SCOPE
Use this module for literary analysis, teaching, revision diagnosis, or craft study when the task requires explaining how a passage works.

Do not use it to:
- force a symbolic meaning that the text does not establish;
- treat author intention as known without evidence;
- identify historical meaning, allusion, textual provenance, or form-law correctness from model memory;
- convert every noticeable feature into a problem to be edited.

Historical, form-specific, cultural, allusive, or textual-status claims remain `SOURCE-GATED` when the supplied/verified evidence is insufficient.

## MECHANISM
1. **Anchor in observable language.** Quote or point to the smallest relevant textual feature.
2. **Relate features.** Ask what changes when a word, structure, rhythm, image, access boundary, or pattern recurs or contrasts.
3. **Infer with calibration.** Separate what is explicit from what is merely plausible.
4. **Test function.** Connect the feature to pressure, orientation, characterization, rhythm, information control, or another demonstrated effect.
5. **Test alternatives.** If more than one reading fits the evidence, retain the live alternatives instead of forcing closure.
6. **Transfer only the mechanism.** Reuse the functional relation, not the source text's surface realization.

## TEXTUAL SIGNALS
Potentially relevant signals include:
- lexical repetition or substitution;
- marked word order or ellipsis;
- sentence-length or punctuation shifts;
- contrast between surrounding norm and a local deviation;
- pronoun/reference patterns;
- shifts in speaker, register, narrator access, or focal frame;
- recurring objects/images whose practical or relational function changes;
- juxtaposition, delay, omission, contradiction, or graded revelation.

A signal is not automatically significant merely because it is countable or unusual.

## EFFECTS
Possible effects include attention, emphasis, hesitation, acceleration, fracture, ambiguity, intimacy/distance, social positioning, information restriction, reinterpretation, or reader expectation.

Name an effect only when a textual mechanism supports it. Do not infer emotion, intention, symbolism, or reliability from one isolated cue when alternatives remain viable.

## CONDITIONS
A close-reading claim is strong when:
- the observation is recoverable from the text;
- the inference strength matches the evidence;
- the proposed function explains more than surface oddity;
- counterevidence has been considered;
- historical/form/source claims are separately verified when required.

## COUNTEREXAMPLES
- A repeated word may be accidental duplication rather than meaningful patterning.
- A fragment may be an unintentional grammar failure rather than pressure-shaped rhythm.
- An empty chair can have several changing practical and relational functions without possessing one fixed symbolic meaning.
- A long sentence can be functional if each clause changes the active decision or relation.
- An apparent POV anomaly may be legitimate broader narrator access if the narrative contract establishes it.

## COMMON ERRORS
- naming an emotion before establishing pressure/evidence;
- treating plausible implicature as explicit content;
- reading recurrence count as proof of symbolism;
- forcing every passage through every analytical lens;
- resolving productive ambiguity merely for clarity;
- explaining a literary effect without pointing to its textual mechanism;
- copying a model object's surface features during craft transfer;
- using author biography, historical claims, or allusion guesses without verified source support.

## HISTORICAL VARIATION
`SOURCE-GATED`.

Close reading may observe historical lexical/syntactic difference, but historical interpretation requires dated textual evidence, reliable scholarship, edition information, or equivalent source support. Do not apply contemporary norms automatically to older texts.

## FORM VARIATION
`SOURCE-GATED BY FORM`.

The observation-to-interpretation discipline is portable, but the significance of meter, rhyme, parallelism, regulated structure, genre convention, or classical rhetoric depends on the relevant form module/source. Do not invent missing form-law.

## CLOSE READING METHOD
Use the smallest route sufficient for the passage:

1. **OBSERVE:** identify 1–3 materially relevant features.
2. **RELATE:** show contrast, recurrence, sequence, or access relation.
3. **INFER:** state what those relations make plausible.
4. **INTERPRET:** explain the effect/meaning the evidence supports.
5. **ALTERNATIVE:** retain another reading if evidence materially supports it.
6. **TRANSFER:** name the reusable mechanism only if the task asks for craft application.

Stop when additional lenses add no material explanatory value.

## CRAFT TRANSFER
Transfer pattern:

`SOURCE FEATURE → FUNCTION → CONDITIONS → NEW SURFACE REALIZATION`

Example mechanism: an ordinary object changes practical and relational function across scenes while one character interprets it symbolically and narration withholds confirmation.

A valid transfer changes object, setting, relationship, diction, syntax, and scene furniture while preserving the mechanism. Surface imitation is a failure.

## DRILLS
1. **Observation-only:** list three textual facts without naming emotion, motive, or theme.
2. **Inference ladder:** label five statements `OBSERVATION / INFERENCE / INTERPRETATION`.
3. **Alternative reading:** produce two readings and identify evidence shared by both plus evidence that differentiates them.
4. **Lens economy:** choose only two lenses that materially explain a short passage and state why the others are unnecessary.
5. **Mechanism transfer:** recreate a functional relation with completely different surface material.

## AI OPERATIONAL RULES
- Evidence before interpretation.
- Observation and inference must remain distinguishable in wording.
- If the task asks for a definitive meaning but the evidence supports multiple readings, use `HOLD-INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` for the definitive verdict and provide bounded alternatives when useful.
- Do not call recurrence, deviation, ambiguity, or opacity a flaw merely because it increases processing effort.
- Do not preserve a feature merely because it is unusual; test function.
- Do not promote author memory, deleted outline material, or unsupported context into textual evidence.
- Historical/form/allusion/provenance claims require source support.
- Craft transfer preserves mechanism, not surface.

## SELF-CHECK
Before finalizing:
- What exactly did I observe?
- Which statements are inference rather than text fact?
- Did I identify a mechanism connecting feature to effect?
- Is there a viable alternative reading I suppressed?
- Did I force an unused analytical lens?
- Did I make a historical/form/source claim without evidence?
- If transferring craft, did I change the surface realization materially?
