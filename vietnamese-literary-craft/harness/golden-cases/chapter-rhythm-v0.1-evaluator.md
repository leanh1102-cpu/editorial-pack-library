# CHAPTER / ARC RHYTHM v0.1 — Frozen Evaluator

STATUS: `FROZEN / EVALUATOR-ONLY`

MAX SCORE: `20`
PROMOTION GATE: `20/20 + HARD FAIL 0`

Score each case 0–2.

- `2`: action + mechanism + structural scope match the expected decision boundary.
- `1`: direction is broadly right but the recommendation is too generic, too broad, or weakly grounded in ordering/change-of-state.
- `0`: mechanism is wrong, the answer misses the multi-scene defect, or a hard fail occurs.

## Global hard fails

Any one of the following is a hard fail:

1. inventing an attack, death, betrayal, accident, deadline, romance beat, clue, or other new canon event solely to create variety or tension;
2. requiring a fixed number of climaxes, action scenes, quiet scenes, aftermath scenes, or beat pattern per chapter/arc;
3. treating low external intensity as structural failure when object/relationship/choice state is changing materially;
4. treating louder prose, harsher insults, violence, or shorter sentences as sufficient arc escalation without changed cost/options/access;
5. deleting necessary aftermath solely to accelerate pacing;
6. rewriting an entire chapter/arc when the defect is confined to one or two scene nodes;
7. prescribing generic “add an action scene / twist / fight” without evidence from the packet;
8. flattening all scene seams into one preferred transition style;
9. demanding monotonic pressure increase with no recovery or irregularity;
10. turning one repeated mechanism diagnosis into a universal ban on dialogue-, inquiry-, domestic-, or quiet scenes.
