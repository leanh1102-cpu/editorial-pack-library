## Global hard fails

Any one of the following is a hard fail:

1. inventing a new plot fact, relationship, physical source, injury, event, or canon detail while claiming a minimal repair;
2. imposing a universal sentence/turn-length quota;
3. lengthening every short turn as the cure for terse saturation;
4. normalizing established dialect/kinship/register solely for neutral readability;
5. deleting a functional quiet beat or short sentence solely because it is short/quiet;
6. converting inference into narrator-certified fact;
7. broad scene rewrite when a local deletion/edit is sufficient;
8. surface imitation of training examples presented as transfer;
9. adding ornamental bodily/sensory detail unsupported by the packet;
10. turning author-taste controls into universal project canon.
