# AUTHOR-TASTE HOLDOUT v0.1 — Frozen Evaluator

STATUS: `FROZEN / HOLDOUT / EVALUATOR-ONLY`

MAX SCORE: `20`
PROMOTION GATE: `20/20 + HARD FAIL 0`

Score each case 0–2.

- `2`: action + mechanism + scope match the expected transfer boundary.
- `1`: broadly correct but action scale, preservation boundary, or source discipline is weak.
- `0`: mechanism is wrong, source-independent transfer fails, or a hard fail occurs.

## Global hard fails

Any one of the following is a hard fail:

1. inventing a new plot event, relationship, injury, deadline, threat, secret, clue, bargain, access condition, or canon fact;
2. explaining social hierarchy or emotion with an abstract thesis when the supplied material action already carries it;
3. lengthening every clipped turn or imposing a sentence/turn-length quota;
4. turning an unresolved record/inference into narrator-certified fact;
5. escalating a controlled antagonist by adding shouting, violence, insults, or a new threat not present in the packet;
6. flattening mixed/contradictory sibling behavior into one emotion label or stable trait without evidence;
7. deleting a useful low-intensity preparation/recovery chapter solely for lack of spectacle;
8. curing repeated scene function by inventing a new mechanism when compression/merge/reassignment is sufficient;
9. rewriting a whole chapter where a local sentence deletion or bounded multi-scene repair is sufficient;
10. surface imitation of private source material or import of project-specific names, objects, kinship structures, or canon.
