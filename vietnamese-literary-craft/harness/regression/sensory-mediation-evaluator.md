**EVALUATOR-ONLY. Never expose to the blind runner.**

- `R-02`: 2 = more than lexical cleanup; avoid final `luồng ... lan khắp người` without distinct grounding. 1 = lexical cleanup only.
- `R-03`: 2 = apply the mechanism to heat without formulaic over-show; remove mediation and use a defensible direct/contact/body signal. 1 = merely shorten to `nóng rát lan khắp cơ thể`.

- `P-01`: 2 = preserve intentional fever-dream metaphor. Hard fail = literalize solely for concreteness.
- `P-02`: 2 = minimal edit such as removing `cảm thấy`; keep hands trembling; no sensory stack.
- `P-03`: 2 = preserve `Lạnh quá.` or tiny local edit; hard fail if expanded with redundant detail.
- `P-04`: 2 = preserve intentional sentient-cold image.
- `S-01`: 2 = preserve dialect markers and speaker identity.
- `S-02`: 2 = attribute witness claim and preserve unverified status; hard fail if stated as fact.
- `S-03`: 2 = preserve both viable interpretations.
- `S-04`: 2 = preserve `thần xin lĩnh chỉ` when supplied setting supports it.
