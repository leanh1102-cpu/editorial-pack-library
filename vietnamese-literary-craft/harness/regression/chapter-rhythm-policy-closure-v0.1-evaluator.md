# CHAPTER / ARC RHYTHM POLICY CLOSURE v0.1 — Frozen Evaluator

STATUS: `FROZEN / EVALUATOR-ONLY`

MAX SCORE: `4`
PROMOTION GATE: `4/4 + HARD FAIL 0`

Score each case 0–2.

- `2`: action + policy boundary + structural scope match.
- `1`: diagnosis is correct but the answer HOLDs the whole task, invents a replacement mechanism, or uses the wrong action scale.
- `0`: core mechanism is wrong or a hard fail occurs.

## Global hard fails

1. inventing a sacrifice, betrayal, attack, deadline, clue, access loss, relationship rupture, asset loss, or other new canon mechanism;
2. requiring a fixed climax or tension pattern;
3. recommending a whole-arc rewrite when compression/merge/reweighting is sufficient;
4. preserving all repeated nodes merely because no replacement mechanism is licensed;
5. treating louder language as genuine structural escalation.

## CRC-01
EXPECTED ACTION: `REVISE`

Full credit requires:
- diagnose repeated payoff mechanism across major climaxes;
- refuse to invent a replacement climax mechanism not supported by the packet;
- still perform bounded structural repair by compressing, merging, de-emphasizing, reweighting, or reassigning repeated payoff nodes;
- recognize that lack of evidence for a *new* mechanism does not force whole-task `HOLD` when an evidence-grounded restructuring remains available.

## CRC-02
EXPECTED ACTION: `REVISE`

Full credit requires:
- diagnose false escalation across repeated confrontations;
- refuse to invent a new crisis/pressure mechanism;
- compress, merge, reweight, or otherwise reduce redundant confrontation nodes using the supplied structure;
- recognize that whole-task `HOLD` is inappropriate because a bounded source-grounded repair remains available.

## Interpretation

A 4/4 closure resolves only the `REVISE vs HOLD` policy boundary when no new mechanism is licensed but structural compression/reweighting remains possible. It does not by itself validate the full Chapter / Arc Rhythm module.
