# Author-Taste Vietnamese Prose v0.1 — Candidate Controls

STATUS: `CANDIDATE / SANITIZED / NOT GOLDEN`

These entries are mechanism candidates distilled from owner-approved prose controls and production-observed editing failures. They are deliberately canon-neutral. They do not reproduce private manuscript text and are not evidence of generalization until holdout validation passes.

---

## AT-VP01 — Functional short opening

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** sentence / scene opening
**OBSERVABLES:** one short concrete sentence establishes time or immediate life rhythm; following prose expands naturally.
**MECHANISM:** brevity creates a real entry beat instead of simulating intensity.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** treating every short sentence as an AI defect or lengthening it without a reader-effect reason.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** preserve when the short sentence has a unique timing/attention function and is not part of a saturation pattern.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a dawn scene begins with one compact household sound cue, then moves into ordinary actions and longer syntax.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** copying the exact sensory surface instead of transferring the timing function.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP02 — Space anchors early into POV body

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** scene opening / description
**OBSERVABLES:** large space narrows to room/threshold, then to the focal character's posture, hands, clothing or contacted object.
**MECHANISM:** setting becomes lived space rather than detached camera coverage.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** panoramic description that delays embodiment; forced body detail when the POV is already anchored.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** do not force a funnel shape when a scene already begins at the correct embodied distance.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a courtyard is introduced through a doorway and immediately becomes the floor, footwear and hand position the focal character must manage.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** turning the mechanism into a mandatory wide-to-close template.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP03 — Environment is doing work

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** description / domestic scene
**OBSERVABLES:** smell, heat, tools, food or damaged objects participate in current work and pressure.
**MECHANISM:** setting carries economy, labor and mood through action rather than decorative inventory.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** sensory list added only to make prose vivid; background detail that never interacts with choice or body.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** sparse description is valid when the active objects already carry enough pressure.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a scorched pot, the amount left in bowls and a hand scraping the bottom reveal shortage while the family prepares to leave.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** manufacturing hardship props in scenes where the packet does not establish them.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP04 — Body history through object

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** characterization / description
**OBSERVABLES:** wear, stitching, asymmetry, adapted clothing or habitual handling shows a bodily history.
**MECHANISM:** object-body relation carries history without naming tragedy or demanding pity.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** narrator explains the symbolic/emotional meaning after the object already shows it.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** direct naming remains valid when clarity or accessibility genuinely requires it.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a modified sleeve and the way fabric moves with breathing reveal a long-standing impairment without explanatory summary.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** inventing scars, injuries or symbolic objects not present in source context.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP05 — Concrete intelligence

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** interiority / POV
**OBSERVABLES:** a character reasons through distance, weight, position, timing, ownership, obligation or visible constraints.
**MECHANISM:** intelligence appears as situated calculation rather than thematic omniscience.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** upgrading practical inference into narrator-certified truth or abstract psychological analysis.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** abstract thought is allowed when the character's knowledge/register supports it.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a teenager anticipates how a crowded threshold and another person's unstable stance could change who gets pushed aside.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** importing a competence profile from one character into another.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP06 — Material-specific sound

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** sentence / sensory transition
**OBSERVABLES:** sound emerges from material + action and often redirects attention.
**MECHANISM:** concrete acoustics perform transition work without explanatory packaging such as “a sound rang out”.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** decorative onomatopoeia, repeated isolated sound words, or impossible material acoustics.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** mediated sound phrasing is valid when POV distance or uncertainty requires it.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a wooden utensil scraping a metal vessel creates the audible bridge from observation back to a family argument.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** treating a fixed sound-word glossary as universal.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP07 — Relational dialogue and face-saving

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** dialogue exchange
**OBSERVABLES:** two speakers frame the same object/event differently because one minimizes exposure while the other invokes social consequence, duty or relationship.
**MECHANISM:** conflict lives in naming, address, implication and social positioning, not only proposition content.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** converting turns into bare question/answer units; adding emotion labels that explain what the exchange already performs.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** clipped turns are valid when pressure, role or deliberate refusal makes the cut itself meaningful.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** one family member calls a worn ceremonial item “just cloth”; another replies by naming what outsiders will notice about it.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** copying kinship terms or social hierarchy from the training source into unrelated settings.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP08 — Silence is built by action

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** dialogue aftermath / quiet beat
**OBSERVABLES:** response is displaced into hands, posture, an object, a failed knot, a repeated household task or delayed movement.
**MECHANISM:** silence carries relationship and pressure through physical continuation.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** narrator first labels silence/emotion and then repeats the same information with action.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** explicit “không đáp” or similar wording remains valid when orientation would otherwise become unclear.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** after a difficult question, a person re-ties a strap one-handed; the crooked knot supplies the emotional/physical residue.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** fetishizing body action so every emotional beat requires a prop.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP09 — Domestic allocation carries cost and relationship

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** domestic scene / stakes
**OBSERVABLES:** portions, fuel, medicine, clothing or labor are allocated unevenly; the choice exposes shortage and relational priority.
**MECHANISM:** material distribution makes care and cost visible without exposition.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** abstract poverty statement where existing household action can carry the same information; invented deprivation for melodrama.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** direct economic information is valid when the reader needs exact terms for later causality.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a caretaker serves equal bowls but quietly keeps the burnt remainder for themself.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** turning every intimate scene into scarcity signaling.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP10 — Scene seam through movement and concrete pressure

**SOURCE_CLASS:** owner-approved prose control
**SCOPE:** scene ending / seam
**OBSERVABLES:** door, route, time limit, unfinished task or another person's waiting pushes the cast toward the next scene.
**MECHANISM:** scene closes on changed state + movement rather than narrator summary or thematic maxim.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** polished concluding sentence that explains the meaning of what just happened.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** reflective endings remain valid when reflection itself is the scene's material turn.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a household exits because someone outside will not wait; the environmental change at the threshold carries the seam.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** forcing every scene to end with physical departure.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

## AT-VP11 — Terse saturation is an exchange-level failure

**SOURCE_CLASS:** production-observed author rewrite + reader-effect complaint
**SCOPE:** dialogue exchange / scene rhythm
**OBSERVABLES:** many individually plausible short turns accumulate into proposition-only ping-pong; kinship, particles, hesitation, social strategy and syntax recovery disappear.
**MECHANISM:** sentence-level correctness can still produce exchange-level deadness; pressure and relationship should govern compression and recovery.
**FAILURE_BOUNDARY:** all characters collapse into the same terse register; low/moderate-pressure scenes remain clipped after the acute pressure has passed.
**PRESERVE_BOUNDARY:** emergency commands, deliberate refusal, shock fragments and short-turn clusters with clear social action remain valid.
**ANONYMIZED_CASE:** a family discusses a payment problem in a calm room using eight one-to-three-word turns with no relational marking; revision restores address, indirectness and uneven turn length without adding plot facts.
**TRANSFER_RISK:** overcorrecting by lengthening every turn, sprinkling particles mechanically, or treating verbosity as naturalness.
**STATUS:** CANDIDATE

- Holdout evidence must come from a source span not used to author these entries.
