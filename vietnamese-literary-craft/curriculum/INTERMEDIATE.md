# Intermediate Curriculum

**Status:** `INTERMEDIATE-INTEGRATED v0.5 / VALIDATED`

## Intermediate spine
1. `I1 POV, Access, and Focalization` — judge access-frame coherence, marked shifts, broader narrator access, and belief-vs-fact boundaries.
2. `I2 Narrative Time` — diagnose order, duration/speed, and frequency by function rather than chronological or compression quotas.
3. `I3 Event, Plot, and Causal Debt` — distinguish event ordering from causality; preserve reconstructable delayed dependencies; do not invent punishment or links.
4. `I4 Dialogue as Social Action` — literal content, indirect action, interruption, hierarchy, register, and anti-stereotype voice differentiation.
5. `I5 Interiority and Narrative Distance` — direct thought, narrated interiority, narrator/character blending, access, and duplication control.
6. `I6 Character State and Behavioral Logic` — baseline tendency plus state, pressure, relation, social purpose, preparation, and aftermath.
7. `I7 Description, Object, Motif, and Space` — selective detail and functional space; recurrence does not equal fixed symbol or causal device.
8. `I8 Scene and Chapter Information Architecture` — fact/belief/claim/inference layers, fair reinterpretation, graded revelation, and causal debt.

## Learning loop
`CASE → ACCESS/STATE/TIME/INFORMATION MAP → FAILURE OR NO MATERIAL FAILURE → MECHANISM → SMALLEST ACTION → PRESERVATION CHECK → TRANSFER`.

## Scope lock
Intermediate is not a complete narratology theory. Historical/form-specific narrative claims remain source-gated. Internal operational heuristics remain labeled as internal evidence when not supported by external sources.
