## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Samples are calibration, not canon and not voice to imitate.
- Select one main craft move before patching.
- Do not stack craft moves to make prose look rich.
- A craft move is valid only if it changes pressure, relation, information, cost, movement, reader breath, or genre promise.
- Do not add lore, canon, backstory, custom, or character history.
- If the issue is structural, hand off to the relevant gate instead of decorating prose.
- End with NEXT NODE.
