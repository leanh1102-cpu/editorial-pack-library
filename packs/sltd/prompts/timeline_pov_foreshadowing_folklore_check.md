## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Do not invent timeline events, folklore, prophecy, ritual, custom, village history, clue, payoff, or canon truth.
- Do not reveal hidden truth early.
- Do not change POV access to solve a scene.
- Track only source-supported planted / paid / held / forgotten-risk items.
- Folklore should alter behavior before explanation.
- Material threads must carry pressure, not decorative motif repetition.
- Planting-density bands are diagnostic, not formulas.
- If current manuscript status or lock is required, read current Notion or exact provided source first.
- If event order or canon truth is unclear, hand off to Canon Guard.
- End with NEXT NODE.
