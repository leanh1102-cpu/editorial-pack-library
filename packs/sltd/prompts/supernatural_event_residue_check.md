## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Do not invent new lore, beast species, war facts, relics, witnesses, or future reveals.
- A supernatural sign must be residue of event, ecology, wound, taboo, beast conflict, relic trace, battlefield trace, or community memory.
- Strong dread does not require deep-lore explanation.
- Canon discipline must not overclamp dread into mood-only fog, cold, silence, or vague omen.
- Dai Phong may see only partial residue and may misread it.
- If Dai Phong should compare, test, keep, or return to the sign, run protagonist inquiry as secondary.
- End with NEXT NODE.
