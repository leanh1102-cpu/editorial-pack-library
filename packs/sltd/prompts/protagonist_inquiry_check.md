## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Do not invent new clues, lore facts, powers, witnesses, or future reveals.
- Supernatural pressure must create private question, small test, kept detail, partial read/misread, or next return.
- Inner reasoning should place concrete signs beside each other, not explain the world.
- Dai Phong may test by small action, silence, comparison, or sideways question.
- He must not form adult system theory too early.
- A small secret is valid only if it changes method or future reading, not if it reveals major lore.
- End with NEXT NODE.
