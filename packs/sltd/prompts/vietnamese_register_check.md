# SLTD Vietnamese Register Check

Use this prompt when prose may be too Chinese-translated, too Hán Việt-heavy, too modern-flat, or missing Viet Dao balance.

This prompt does not authorize canon term changes or new terminology.

## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Do not remove established canon terms.
- Do not invent new Vietnamese replacements for locked terms.
- Do not remove all Hán Việt as a rule.
- Keep Hán Việt for canon, law, rite, rank, old taboo, Dao pressure, or formal titles when earned.
- Lower decorative abstract terms into scene-native Vietnamese action.
- Use calibration samples before making patch choices.
- If terms are canon-locked, hand off to Canon Guard.
- If line rhythm is the main issue, hand off to Vietnamese Line Surgery.
- End with NEXT NODE.
