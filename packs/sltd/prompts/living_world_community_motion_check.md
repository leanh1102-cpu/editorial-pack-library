# SLTD Living World & Community Motion Check

Use this prompt when setting, community, custom, social habit, or environment may be passive backdrop rather than active scene force.

This prompt recommends world-motion repair. It does not authorize canon invention or new worldbuilding systems.

```text
manifest.yml

```

## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Do not invent major lore, custom, institution, ritual, faction, village history, or canon geography.
- If evidence is missing, mark WORLD MOTION GAP rather than inventing worldbuilding.
- Environment must change action, relation, cost, access, witness, timing, or choice.
- Custom or taboo should appear through behavior before explanation.
- Prefer small butterfly traces over large invented consequences.
- If repair depends on canon geography, custom, social structure, or history, hand off to Canon Guard.
- If the scene lacks pressure, hand off to First-Pass Editorial Workflow or Scene-First Prose Judgment.
- End with NEXT NODE.
