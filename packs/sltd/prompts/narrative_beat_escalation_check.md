## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Do not add major plot, lore, faction, power, or named character.
- Do not reveal hidden canon early.
- Do not copy benchmark novels.
- A beat must change pressure, information, position, cost, or promise.
- Off-POV / parallel conflict must return as pressure, cost, rumor, resource shift, access shift, witness change, delayed danger, misunderstanding, deadline, or proof.
- Interruption must leave inherited pressure.
- Peak must leave result, cost, changed state, or promise/payoff movement.
- If the issue is cadence rather than beat structure, hand off to Dynamic Range or Line Surgery.
- End with NEXT NODE.
