## Rules

- Do not judge exact dialogue without exact source surface.
- Do not invent voice habits or relationship history.
- A character physically present must remain present through action, silence with reason, reaction, or decision.
- Dialogue must sound like the speaker, not like the chapter function.
- Relationship change must have trace: what happened before, what changes now, what remains unsaid.
- Location must be a stage with layout and usable objects, not a backdrop.
- Comedy must stay inside tone. Grief or realization must not be rushed or over-explained.
- Use natural Vietnamese sound and physical detail; do not force comic-style effects into SLTD.
- End with NEXT_NODE.
