## Rules

- Do not patch without exact source text.
- Do not use Notion labels as prose.
- Do not invent growth, power, lore, system knowledge, insight, backstory, or future route.
- A protagonist gain must be shown through object, body, relation, witness/social trace, cost, knowledge limit, and aftershock.
- Gain type must not appear as a prose label.
- C030 low-layer lore is valid when it becomes world-rule sensitivity for Dai Phong, not system knowledge.
- If objects become proof tags, hand off to Prose Craft or Anti-AI Composite.
- If behavior is forced, hand off to Character Agency.
- If the protagonist knows too much, hand off to Timeline / POV.
- End with NEXT NODE.
