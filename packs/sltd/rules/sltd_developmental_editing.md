# SLTD Developmental Editing

Use this rule before line edits when a chapter or cluster has story-level risk.

## Scope

Developmental editing checks story function, not sentence beauty.

It asks:

- What is the chapter job?
- What changed by the end?
- What question pulls the reader forward?
- What cost was paid?
- What promise was planted, paid, or left hanging?
- What should not be revealed yet?

## Chapter function checks

A chapter must deliver at least one clear function:

- survival cost;
- social pressure;
- knowledge gain with price;
- mystery pressure;
- relationship shift;
- object payoff;
- route or access change;
- new risk created by a small win.

## Red flags

- chapter has events but no changed state;
- scene explains lore before the household or body feels it;
- protagonist gains without a cost or witness;
- reader question is abstract rather than concrete;
- cluster repeats the same lesson with new objects;
- payoff is delayed without pressure.

## Repair rule

Fix story function before line-level polish.

If the chapter lacks changed state, add consequence within source lock. Do not add major canon.
