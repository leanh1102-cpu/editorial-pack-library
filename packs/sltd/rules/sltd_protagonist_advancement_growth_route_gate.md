# SLTD Protagonist Advancement Node & Growth Route Calibration

This gate controls protagonist growth as a source-bound advancement route. It is designed first for Dai Phong.

## Core rule

A protagonist gain must be shown through scene evidence: object, body, relation, witness, cost, knowledge limit, and aftershock.

```text
PROTAGONIST_ADVANCEMENT_NODE = one scene/chapter unit where the protagonist gains a specific capacity, limit, debt, sensitivity, or method
GAIN_TARGET = what the protagonist can now notice, endure, choose, hide, read, trade, refuse, or survive
GAIN_TYPE = body / perception / social reading / language-record / resource logic / concealment-silence / moral-Dao pressure / world-rule sensitivity
COST = what the gain takes from body, family, relation, safety, time, food, money, trust, witness, name, or future route
KNOWLEDGE_LIMIT = what the protagonist can know, infer, misread, hide, or must not know yet
FORBIDDEN_GAIN = growth the source does not allow yet: adult reasoning, clean power-up, system knowledge, hidden lore, moral lecture, sudden competence
GROWTH_ROUTE = cumulative ladder of source-bound gains across scenes, chapters, packets, and arcs
```

Do not turn scene objects into proof items for a growth label.

Do not turn lore into a protagonist gain unless the protagonist changes behavior, risk, relation, method, or reader promise.

Do not upgrade intelligence, power, or Dao insight without source evidence and visible cost.

## Use when

- the user asks about Dai Phong's development path, growth route, competence route, power route, intelligence, or protagonist appeal;
- lore appears but does not clearly become protagonist growth;
- Notion labels a scene/chapter as pass but the protagonist gain is unclear;
- objects, people, body traces, witnesses, or lore details appear as editorial labels rather than lived prose;
- a scene risks making Dai Phong too adult, too passive, too lucky, too cleanly powerful, or too concept-aware;
- a chapter needs to answer what Dai Phong gained, what he paid, what he still cannot know, and what returns later.

## Gain types

```text
BODY = survives, endures, detects limits, learns what cold, hunger, breath, illness, pain, or fatigue can and cannot do
PERCEPTION = reads objects, traces, silence, sound, smell, body state, animal behavior, mud, ash, water, paper, door, scale, tool, stone
SOCIAL_READING = reads who avoids, who witnesses, who repeats wrong, who can deny, who holds social weight, who can make a lie stand
LANGUAGE_RECORD = reads word, name, mark, tally, account, old paper, household phrase, village saying, register, pass, route, or record
RESOURCE_LOGIC = learns price, exchange, debt, food, medicine, labor, tool, coin, paper, route, or cultivation resource as pressure
CONCEALMENT_SILENCE = learns when to ask, not ask, hide, misdirect, keep a detail, or force another person to expose a position
MORAL_DAO_PRESSURE = learns that survival, debt, family, witness, and truth do not align cleanly
WORLD_RULE_SENSITIVITY = senses a low-level world rule through objects, body, family fear, paper, naming, debt, or witness before understanding high lore
```

Gain type is not a prose label. It must be embodied by scene movement.

## C030 low-lore rule

C030 may open low-layer lore as a default rule of the world. Do not treat lore presence as failure by default.

For C030, valid growth is not system knowledge. It is world-rule sensitivity.

A cold stone, medicine bowl, Thuy Hoa's hand, Dai Son's breath, a dog falling silent at the wrong time, old paper, Lao Hoac avoiding a word, a name being called wrong, or an object placed in the house may support growth only when they change household behavior, risk, relation, or Dai Phong's future reading method.

Do not drag these objects into prose as functions. They must remain work, fear, habit, illness, avoidance, cost, and pressure first.

## Growth route ladder

```text
breath / body survival
small object and trace reading
food, medicine, price, exchange, and debt
word, mark, scale, name, and record
rumor, witness, avoidance, and community behavior
household memory, old paper, route, and hidden object
village status, tax, labor, household registration, pass, and gate
cultivation resource value only after source permits visible mechanism and cost
```

A later gain must inherit cost and method from earlier gains. Do not reset competence.

## Safe repair

Safe repair may identify a source-supported gain, reduce lore label into visible object/body/relation/witness pressure, move growth from narration into action/misread/silence/cost/aftershock, or mark a proposed gain as blocked when source, reveal window, cost, or visible sign is missing.

Safe repair must not invent new power, clue, lore, system, insight, or route; make Dai Phong adult-conscious too early; make him win by explanation or clean genius; turn every object into a symbolic proof item; or use Notion labels as prose.

## Patch rule

If exact source text is available:

```text
OLD:
ADVANCEMENT FAILURE:
WHY THE GAIN IS UNCLEAR / LABELLED / TOO CLEAN:
SELECTED GAIN TARGET:
NEW:
WHAT OBJECT / BODY / RELATION / WITNESS NOW CARRIES THE GAIN:
COST:
KNOWLEDGE LIMIT:
AFTERSHOCK:
RISK:
```

If exact source text is missing, do not patch. Run Source Surface Check.
