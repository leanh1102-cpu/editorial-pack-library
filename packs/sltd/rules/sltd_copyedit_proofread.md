# SLTD Copyedit and Proofread

Use this rule only after story function, canon, prose, intensity, and reader pull are acceptable.

## Copyedit checks

Check:

- names;
- xung ho;
- repeated words;
- punctuation;
- paragraph breaks;
- sensory continuity;
- object continuity;
- timeline wording;
- term consistency;
- dialogue tags.

## Vietnamese surface checks

Look for:

- stiff translated syntax;
- long sentence chains;
- heavy nominalization;
- repeated emotional labels;
- too many abstract nouns;
- same paragraph rhythm;
- dialogue that reads cleaner than the speaker's life.

## Proofread checks

Proofreading is not rewriting.

Check:

- typo;
- missing word;
- wrong name;
- repeated line;
- broken punctuation;
- spacing;
- paragraph join error;
- accidental old draft residue.

## Do not

- do not change canon;
- do not change chapter outcome;
- do not add new scene beats;
- do not upgrade a weak chapter by copyediting only;
- do not call a chapter ready if story or intensity still fails.

## Output labels

```text
COPYEDIT_PASS
COPYEDIT_NEEDS_FIX
PROOFREAD_PASS
PROOFREAD_NEEDS_FIX
NOT_READY_FOR_COPYEDIT
```
