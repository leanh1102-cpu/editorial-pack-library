# SLTD Character Distinctiveness & Iceberg Profile Gate

This gate prevents characters from becoming interchangeable scene functions.

It extends Character Agency. Agency asks whether a character is forced by the plot. Distinctiveness asks whether the character remains themselves beyond the scene function.

## Core rule

A character must not be only a role in the scene.

```text
CHARACTER DISTINCTIVENESS = voice + body + habit + relation + concealment + private pressure + visible trace
ICEBERG PROFILE = what the character does not say but carries into behavior
SCENE FUNCTION = what the scene needs from the character
SAFE CHARACTER = scene function passes through a specific person, not a replaceable name
```

If two character names can be swapped without changing the scene, mark character flattening.

## Use when

- characters feel skimmed, flattened, or interchangeable;
- main and side characters serve scene/chapter function rather than living through it;
- everyone shares the same tone, expression, gesture, silence, clothing use, or reaction pattern;
- a character has no private want, private fear, concealment strategy, or relation-specific behavior;
- side characters enter only to ask, block, explain, witness, or deliver a beat;
- a character's object, symbol, clothing, or body detail is a label rather than a behavioral force;
- dialogue voice collapse appears across the cast;
- the user says nhân vật bị lướt, làm nền cho cảnh, đổi tên vẫn dùng được, thiếu phần chìm, or tảng băng trôi.

## Authority

This gate sits below:

```text
current user instruction
current Notion source or user-provided source packet
source fidelity gate
canon guard
evidence discipline
role boundary contracts
```

It must not invent trauma, backstory, hidden motive, symbol, title, relationship history, or canon profile.

If evidence is missing, mark the gap instead of inventing depth.

## Interchangeability test

Ask:

```text
If this character's name is replaced with another cast member, what breaks?
Does the dialogue still fit too easily?
Does the gesture belong to this person or to generic prose?
Does the object/clothing change behavior or only identify the person?
What is this character hiding, avoiding, or managing that the scene does not fully explain?
Does the character bring pressure into the scene, or only respond when the scene asks?
```

If nothing breaks, mark interchangeable character risk.

## Iceberg test

A character's hidden layer should appear through present behavior, not biography exposition.

Ask:

```text
What remains unsaid?
What behavior hints at it?
What does the character refuse, delay, misread, over-control, or avoid?
What object, clothing, posture, work habit, or relation pressure carries the hidden layer?
What cost is personal to this character after the scene moves on?
```

If the hidden layer is only explained in narration, mark backstory explained not embodied.

## Voice and behavior test

Distinctiveness may appear through:

- sentence length and directness;
- avoidance, interruption, or delayed answer;
- choice of practical words;
- who the character looks at or refuses to look at;
- how they handle objects, clothing, tools, animals, doors, bowls, medicine, paper, or weapons;
- what they notice first under pressure;
- what they never say in front of a specific person;
- how their body betrays or hides fear.

Do not give everyone a decorative quirk.

A signature must change behavior, relation, choice, or cost.

## Safe repair

Safe repair may:

- adjust dialogue toward source-supported voice, relation, age, work, or fear;
- add small refusal, delay, avoidance, misread, or concealment when source supports it;
- make an existing object, clothing item, posture, work habit, or body detail affect behavior;
- give a side character a small practical want inside the scene;
- personalize cost after a choice;
- let hidden pressure appear through action rather than explanation;
- make relation-specific behavior visible.

Safe repair must not:

- invent trauma or backstory;
- invent a new motive or secret;
- create decorative quirks;
- assign symbolic objects without source basis;
- change canon relationships;
- make every character louder or more dramatic;
- let a side character steal the scene's function;
- overwrite exact source surface without permission.

## Handoff

If plot is forcing behavior, hand off to Character Agency Check.

If distinctiveness depends on missing canon or relationship history, hand off to Canon Guard.

If voice is the only blocker, hand off to Vietnamese Line Surgery or Dialogue Voice Calibration.

If a side character needs scene function clarification, hand off to Story Doctor.
