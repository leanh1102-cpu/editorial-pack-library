# SLTD Webnovel Momentum Benchmark

This rule checks whether a chapter or packet has strong webnovel pull.

It is not a claim that SLTD equals or surpasses any outside work. It is an internal pressure test for reader momentum, payoff, and reread value.

## Use when

- the user asks whether a chapter, packet, or arc is compelling;
- the user asks for batch or packet audit;
- the user compares SLTD ambition to top-tier webnovel standards;
- the user asks why a chapter feels correct but not addictive;
- the user asks for REDTEAM, PREMORTEM, READINESS, or NODETRACE over a chapter packet.

## Source rule

For current-state claims, read current Notion source or user-provided current packet.

Do not use legacy Workdecks as current manuscript unless the user names them as current source.

## Benchmark axes

Score each axis from 0 to 5.

```text
0 = absent or damaging
1 = weak
2 = present but thin
3 = functional
4 = strong
5 = exceptional
```

### 1. Opening hook

Does the chapter pull the reader within the first 1-3 paragraphs?

Check:

- immediate pressure;
- concrete situation;
- unanswered question;
- no lore-frontloading;
- mobile readability.

### 2. Scene pressure

Does each scene have one main pressure that changes behavior?

Check:

- someone wants something now;
- someone cannot say the full truth;
- an object or body condition forces action;
- the scene does not exist only to explain.

### 3. Reader reward

Does the chapter pay the reader with a discovery, turn, cost, competence display, emotional hit, or tactical reversal?

Check:

- the reader receives something worth the page time;
- the reward is not only lore;
- the reward changes the next chapter.

### 4. Mystery loop

Does the chapter open or sustain a question that makes the reader want the next chapter?

Check:

- mystery is tied to concrete pressure;
- no fake withholding;
- no over-answering;
- the chapter knows what not to reveal.

### 5. Cost and consequence

Does the protagonist or another key character pay a visible cost?

Check:

- cost is physical, social, moral, relational, material, or tactical;
- the cost remains after the scene;
- consequence is not summarized away.

### 6. Protagonist competence

Does Dai Phong solve or survive through observation, restraint, trade, memory, body, tools, or relationship pressure?

Check:

- no author rescue;
- no clean genius label;
- competence is visible in action;
- mistakes still matter.

### 7. Antagonistic force

Is there an active force pushing back?

Check:

- person, system, weather, debt, illness, hunger, rumor, law, beast, sect, or terrain;
- pressure changes tactics;
- opposition has teeth.

### 8. Payoff chain

Does the chapter connect backward and forward?

Check:

- earlier setup pays off;
- new debt or promise is created;
- repeated motifs gain force, not decoration;
- chapter ending makes the next chapter necessary.

### 9. Prose pull

Does the prose keep the eye moving on mobile?

Check:

- sentence rhythm varies;
- action breaks dialogue;
- concrete objects carry pressure;
- no symbolic label leakage;
- no clean AI transition habit.

### 10. Distinctive SLTD flavor

Does the chapter feel like SLTD rather than generic cultivation fiction?

Check:

- daily life interrupts lore;
- folk pressure, body, object, hunger, cold, debt, family, village, sect machinery, or survival trade anchors the scene;
- mystery stays behind lived pressure;
- author taste is respected.

## Packet-level checks

For 3-10 chapter packet:

- drop-off risk;
- repeated scene shape;
- promise/payoff debt;
- protagonist agency drift;
- lore density spike;
- weak chapter ending;
- missing emotional anchor;
- antagonist pressure gap;
- rhythm fatigue;
- false readiness.

For 30 chapter arc:

- escalation ladder;
- antagonist ladder;
- relationship cost;
- mystery economy;
- world expansion through pressure;
- reader memory anchors;
- arc-level payoff;
- publication blocker.

## Verdict bands

```text
45-50 = TOP-TIER PULL CANDIDATE
38-44 = STRONG WEBNOVEL MOMENTUM
30-37 = FUNCTIONAL BUT PATCH NEEDED
20-29 = UNDERREACHED
0-19 = STRUCTURAL REWORK NEEDED
```

A high score is not a publication lock. Publication lock still requires current source evidence and human pass.

## Safety

- Do not compare against named external novels as factual ranking unless the user provides a defined rubric.
