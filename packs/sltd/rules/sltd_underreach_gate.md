# SLTD Underreach Gate

This gate catches scenes that are correct but do not hit hard enough.

## Not enough for PASS

A scene does not pass only because it is:

- canon-safe;
- grammatically clean;
- free of obvious AI phrases;
- furnished with objects;
- lightly hooked.

## Required pressure

At least two of these must be present:

- cost;
- changed state;
- near consequence;
- someone sees, remembers, suspects, or avoids;
- object changes position, owner, use, value, or risk;
- a hard choice costs something;
- mystery creates a new concrete question;
- body pressure changes behavior.

## Underreach labels

Use these labels:

```text
SAFE_BUT_THIN
CLEAN_BUT_NOT_SHARP
OBJECT_PRESENT_BUT_NO_COST
MYSTERY_PRESENT_BUT_NO_PRESSURE
GREEN_PASS_ILLUSION
NO_CHANGED_STATE
LOW_READER_REWARD
```

## Green pass illusion

If metadata says pass but the chapter lacks Human Chapter Pass, Publication Lock, or packet-level read, do not call it locked.

## Repair direction

Do not add major canon to fix underreach. Add pressure through:

- body;
- money;
- debt;
- household object;
- witness;
- rumor;
- wrong naming;
- blocked access;
- a small loss;
- a choice with cost.
