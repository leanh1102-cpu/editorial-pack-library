# SLTD Timeline, POV, Foreshadowing & Folklore Thread Gate

This gate manages time position, event order, POV boundary, reader knowledge, foreshadowing ledger, folklore mutation, and material planting density across scene, chapter, packet, and arc.

It is a thread-management layer. It does not replace Narrative Beat, Living World, Prose Craft, Character Distinctiveness, or Canon Guard.

## Core rule

Threads must be planted, held, paid, or retired with evidence.

```text
TIMELINE = where the scene sits in event order, elapsed time, and consequence heat
POV BOUNDARY = what the viewpoint holder can know, infer, misread, hide, or miss
READER KNOWLEDGE = what the reader knows beyond the POV and why that gap creates pressure
FORESHADOWING LEDGER = planted / paid / held / forgotten-risk items tracked by source evidence
FOLKLORE THREAD = rumor, taboo, proverb, song, custom, misheard phrase, ritual trace, or community behavior that mutates through social motion
MATERIAL THREAD = recurring object, place, animal, weather, work, food, medicine, debt, road, paper, clothing, tool, or sound that carries pressure across scenes
PLANTING DENSITY = how many thread signals a scene/chapter carries before it becomes overloaded or decorative
```

Do not plant without a route to payoff or pressure.

Do not pay off what was not planted or earned.

Do not let the narrator know what the POV cannot know unless the source intentionally shifts access.

## Use when

- timeline, event order, elapsed time, or consequence heat is unclear;
- POV knowledge leaks, narrator knows too much, or reader access is unmanaged;
- off-POV information appears without a return route;
- foreshadowing, planted objects, clues, names, rumors, or promises risk being forgotten;
- folklore, taboo, proverb, song, misheard phrase, custom, ritual trace, or community behavior needs thread tracking;
- material such as stone, medicine, dog, door, mud, old paper, road, stove, debt, clothes, or tools recurs across scenes;
- scene/chapter/packet planting density feels too thin, too dense, or decorative;
- the user asks about timeline, POV, phục bút, folklore, chất liệu, cài cắm, gieo-trả-treo, or packet thread management.

## Authority

This gate sits below:

```text
current user instruction
current Notion source or user-provided source packet
source fidelity gate
canon guard
evidence discipline
role boundary contracts
```

It must not invent canon, create new folklore, add backstory, add lore, add prophecy, change event order, reveal hidden truth early, or rewrite source without permission.

If current manuscript state, timeline, or lock status is required, read current Notion or exact provided source first.

## Timeline ledger

For each scene/chapter in scope, identify:

```text
TIME POSITION:
EVENT ORDER:
ELAPSED TIME:
CONSEQUENCE HEAT: hot / warm / cooled / forgotten-risk
WHAT HAS ALREADY HAPPENED:
WHAT HAS NOT HAPPENED YET:
WHAT CANNOT BE KNOWN YET:
TIMELINE RISK:
```

Consequence heat asks whether a wound, rumor, cost, debt, fear, clue, or social reaction is still active enough to affect behavior.

## POV boundary test

Ask:

```text
Who is the POV holder or access center?
What can this person directly see, hear, touch, know, infer, misread, or hide?
What can the reader know that the POV holder does not?
Is the knowledge gap creating pressure or solving the scene too early?
Does narration reveal motive, cause, folklore truth, or event order beyond current access?
```

If the narrator explains beyond access without intentional design, mark POV boundary leak.

## Foreshadowing ledger

Track only source-supported items:

```text
PLANTED:
SOURCE SURFACE:
THREAD TYPE: object / phrase / rumor / taboo / behavior / wound / debt / relation / place / weather / animal / paper / tool
PAYOFF ROUTE:
PAID:
HELD:
RISK OF FORGETTING:
NEXT PRESSURE:
```

A held item must still create pressure, uncertainty, cost, altered behavior, or reader promise.

If it no longer does work, retire it, pay it, or mark forgotten-risk.

## Folklore thread test

Folklore should move through community behavior before explanation.

Identify:

```text
FORM: rumor / taboo / proverb / song / custom / misheard phrase / ritual trace / avoidance / child's rhyme
SOURCE IN SCENE:
WHO BELIEVES:
WHO DOUBTS:
WHO REPEATS IT WRONG:
WHAT BEHAVIOR CHANGES:
WHAT IS TRUE / HALF-TRUE / FALSE / UNKNOWN:
MUTATION STEP:
NEXT RETURN ROUTE:
```

Folklore must not become lore lecture or decorative local color.

A good folklore thread changes speech, avoidance, witness behavior, timing, path choice, object handling, or social cost.

## Material planting density

Use soft warning bands:

```text
Scene level:
- one primary pressure anchor
- zero to one clear foreshadowing plant
- one active material layer
- folklore appears as behavior, phrase, avoidance, or residue, not lecture

Chapter level:
- one to two thread movements
- one planted / paid / held item made explicit in ledger
- changed state visible
- POV boundary intact

Packet level:
- GIEO / TRẢ / TREO / NGUY CƠ QUÊN ledger
- timeline continuity checked
- folklore mutation or return checked
- material recurrence checked for pressure, not motif decoration
```

These are diagnostic bands, not formulas.

Do not turn every object, phrase, or custom into a clue.

## Safe repair

Safe repair may:

- clarify event order or consequence heat already present in the source;
- mark what the POV holder can and cannot know;
- move an existing clue, phrase, rumor, object, or material layer into a ledger;
- turn a held thread into pressure through behavior, avoidance, cost, witness, delay, or rumor mutation;
- reduce decorative planting that has no route to payoff;
- identify a next return route for folklore or material threads;
- hand off to Canon Guard when event order, folklore truth, geography, or social custom is uncertain.

Safe repair must not:

- invent timeline events;
- invent folklore, prophecy, ritual, custom, or village history;
- add new clues or payoff without source support;
- reveal hidden truth early;
- change POV access to solve a scene;
- overload a scene with plants;
- turn every material detail into foreshadowing;
- update Notion, GitHub, or manuscript source without explicit permission.

## Handoff

If event order or canon truth is unclear, hand off to Canon Guard.

If the issue is promise/payoff or off-POV conflict returning as pressure, hand off to Narrative Beat Escalation & Aftershock Check.

If folklore moves through community behavior, hand off to Living World & Community Motion Check.

If material layer is a craft problem, hand off to Prose Craft / Style / Material Check.

If chapter or packet reader unit is affected, hand off to Chapter Assembly & Split Check or packet review.
