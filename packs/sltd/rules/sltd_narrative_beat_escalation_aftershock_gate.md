# SLTD Narrative Beat Escalation & Aftershock Gate

This gate controls narrative beat rhythm, anchor points, conflict escalation, off-POV or parallel conflict, interruption, result, cost, and aftershock.

It prevents chapters from becoming a flat chain of events where each beat happens but does not raise pressure, change state, or leave consequence.

## Core rule

A beat is not an event. A beat must change pressure, information, position, cost, or promise.

```text
BEAT = action / information / choice / conflict unit that changes reading pressure
ANCHOR = concrete point that holds the beat: object, debt, witness, deadline, taboo, path, resource, clue, relation, wound
ESCALATION = the next beat makes the situation harder, stranger, more costly, more public, or less reversible
AFTERSHOCK = trace left after interruption, turn, peak, or chapter end
```

Do not stack events without escalation.

Do not interrupt a conflict unless the interruption leaves pressure.

Do not peak without result, cost, or changed state.

## Use when

- beats feel even, flat, or mechanically arranged;
- conflict starts but is interrupted without aftershock;
- a peak happens but changes nothing;
- an off-POV or parallel conflict appears but does not return as pressure;
- a chapter has activities but no pressure ladder;
- a cliffhanger is false because no cost, result, or promise is created;
- story momentum depends on vague danger rather than anchor, cost, and consequence;
- the user asks about beat rhythm, bùng xung đột, cao trào, hậu chấn, conflict ngoài POV, parallel event, promise/payoff, Quỷ bí chi chủ, or Cổ chân nhân benchmark.

## Authority

This gate sits below:

```text
current user instruction
current Notion source or user-provided source packet
source fidelity gate
canon guard
evidence discipline
role boundary contracts
```

It must not add new plot, reveal hidden canon early, import plot rhythm from another novel, or change locked outcome.

Benchmark works may be used only as mechanical reading checks, not as models to copy.

## Beat map

For each scene/chapter in scope, map:

```text
BEAT:
ANCHOR:
PRESSURE CHANGE:
INFORMATION CHANGE:
POSITION CHANGE:
COST / RISK:
PROMISE CREATED / PAID / HELD:
AFTERSHOCK:
NEXT BEAT LINK:
```

A good beat should change at least one of pressure, information, position, cost, or promise.

## Anchor test

Ask:

```text
What concrete thing holds this beat?
Is it a visible object, debt, body cost, rumor, witness, deadline, taboo, road, resource, clue, relation, or wound?
If the anchor is removed, does the beat still matter?
Can the anchor return later as cost, proof, misunderstanding, or leverage?
```

If no concrete anchor exists, mark conflict without anchor.

## Escalation ladder test

Ask:

```text
Does each beat make the problem harder, clearer, stranger, more public, more costly, or less reversible?
Does pressure increase or only continue?
Does a beat change the choice available to a character?
Does a beat create a new witness, debt, wound, suspicion, delay, or lost option?
```

If pressure remains level across several beats, mark uniform beat rhythm.

## Interruption and aftershock test

If a conflict is interrupted, ask:

```text
What sentence was left unfinished?
What object was misplaced, hidden wrong, broken, cooled, moved, or exposed?
Who saw or heard the wrong part?
What cost increases because the conflict stopped early?
What must the next scene inherit?
```

Interruption without inherited pressure is a false cut.

## Peak and result test

If a beat peaks, ask:

```text
What state cannot return to what it was?
Who gained advantage?
Who lost position, safety, money, trust, time, body strength, access, or secrecy?
Who now knows, suspects, lies, or watches?
What promise was paid, delayed, or made more dangerous?
```

Peak without result is spectacle, not story movement.

## Off-POV / parallel conflict rule

Off-POV or parallel conflict is allowed only when it returns as one of:

```text
pressure
cost
rumor
resource shift
changed access
changed witness
delayed danger
misunderstanding
deadline
proof / false proof
```

If it never returns, cut it, shorten it, or mark it as decoration.

## Benchmark guard

Use external webnovel benchmarks only as mechanical checks:

```text
Mystery benchmark:
- What question is planted?
- What object, word, trace, or phenomenon holds the clue?
- Is the answer delayed fairly?
- Does new knowledge carry cost or risk?

Survival / resource benchmark:
- Did the character win by calculation or luck?
- What resource became advantage?
- Who paid for that advantage?
- Is the survival logic cold, clear, and consistent with SLTD canon?
```

Do not copy systems, institutions, motifs, characters, worldview, or plot rhythm from other novels.

## Safe repair

Safe repair may:

- sharpen an existing anchor;
- make a cost already seeded visible;
- move a witness, rumor, object, debt, wound, or deadline into the beat chain;
- convert interruption into inherited pressure;
- make off-POV conflict return through rumor, access, resource, witness, or delayed danger;
- clarify what is gained, lost, known, suspected, or owed;
- reduce decorative conflict that has no payoff.

Safe repair must not:

- add major plot;
- add new lore, faction, power, or named character;
- reveal hidden canon early;
- copy benchmark novels;
- make every scene a climax;
- inflate quiet chapters beyond their function;
- overwrite exact source surface without permission.

## Handoff

If the issue is line cadence rather than beat structure, hand off to Dynamic Range or Line Surgery.

If the issue is missing living-world trace, hand off to Living World & Community Motion Check.

If the issue is character choice or cost, hand off to Character Agency or Character Distinctiveness Check.

If the issue is chapter reader unit or split, hand off to Chapter Assembly & Split Check.
