# SLTD Living World & Community Motion Gate

This gate prevents setting, community, custom, and daily life from becoming passive backdrop.

It checks whether the world keeps moving outside the current POV and whether environment, custom, rumor, work, taboo, and community pressure push back on the characters.

## Core rule

The world must not wait for the protagonist to enter.

```text
LIVING WORLD = place + work + custom + rumor + witness + weather + object + social habit moving beyond POV
COMMUNITY MOTION = what people keep doing, avoiding, mishearing, trading, hiding, or repeating when the scene is not looking
WORLD ICEBERG = social rules, old fears, habits, and taboos carried by behavior before explanation
ENVIRONMENT PUSHBACK = space, weather, animal, object, crowd, road, door, stove, market, field, or forest changing action or cost
```

A location is not ready because it is described.

A custom is not ready because it is named.

A community is not ready because people appear as witnesses.

## Use when

- setting reads like stage backdrop;
- community exists only when POV enters;
- custom, taboo, ritual, rumor, or social habit is decorative;
- scenery labels mood instead of changing action;
- a scene's world stops outside protagonist movement;
- local life does not continue behind the scene;
- a small event leaves no butterfly trace in household, village, road, market, rumor, animal behavior, work, or taboo;
- the user asks about tả cảnh ngụ tình, hiệu ứng cánh bướm, tảng băng trôi của thế giới, community motion, or world outside POV.

## Authority

This gate sits below:

```text
current user instruction
current Notion source or user-provided source packet
source fidelity gate
canon guard
evidence discipline
role boundary contracts
```

It must not invent major lore, new custom, village history, social institution, faction, ritual system, or canon event.

If living-world evidence is missing, mark the gap instead of inventing worldbuilding.

## Backdrop test

Ask:

```text
If the POV character leaves, what keeps happening?
What work was already underway before the scene began?
What habit, taboo, price, rumor, weather, animal, road, tool, or household routine changes behavior?
Does the place make action harder, slower, more public, more costly, or more dangerous?
Does the community mishear, remember, avoid, gossip, watch, hide, trade, or repeat anything?
Does the setting carry pressure, or only mood?
```

If the answer is nothing, mark setting-as-backdrop risk.

## Butterfly trace test

A small event should leave a small trace when appropriate:

```text
object changes handling
rumor mutates one step
animal changes behavior
child repeats a wrong phrase
neighbor notices a door, smoke, mud, silence, or delay
work is interrupted
medicine cools
market price, debt, or access shifts
someone avoids a path
someone cleans, hides, moves, or covers a thing
```

Do not force a big consequence. Prefer a local trace that can reappear later.

## Tả cảnh ngụ tình rule

Landscape should not announce emotion.

Prefer scene-native pressure:

```text
BAD: clouds signal coming disaster
BETTER: rain leaves mud that keeps a footprint, smoke that fails to rise, medicine that cools, a road that becomes watched
```

Environment should embody pressure by changing action, relation, cost, access, witness, or timing.

## Community and custom rule

A custom, taboo, or social habit should affect behavior before it is explained.

Use:

- someone not saying a name;
- adults changing topic when children ask;
- a door left half-open or closed too early;
- a path avoided before the reason is given;
- market speech becoming shorter;
- a witness pretending not to see;
- a tool or object handled in an inherited way.

Do not turn custom into lore lecture.

## Safe repair

Safe repair may:

- add a source-supported daily action already underway;
- make an existing object, place, animal, weather detail, or work task alter action;
- add a small witness, rumor mutation, avoidance, delay, or social cost when source supports it;
- let custom or taboo appear through behavior before explanation;
- show offscreen motion through residue: mud, smoke, cooled medicine, moved stool, shut door, watched path, unfinished work;
- connect community pressure to character choice without changing plot.

Safe repair must not:

- invent major lore, custom, institution, ritual, faction, or history;
- add noisy crowd texture that weakens scene pressure;
- make scenery symbolic before action earns it;
- solve mystery through worldbuilding explanation;
- override canon geography or social structure;
- turn every detail into foreshadowing.

## Patch rule

If exact source text is available:

```text

WHY THE WORLD FEELS STATIC:
NEW:
WHAT NOW MOVES OUTSIDE POV:
WHAT PUSHES BACK ON CHARACTER:

```

If exact source text is missing, do not patch. Run Source Surface Check.

## Handoff

If the issue is missing scene pressure, hand off to First-Pass Editorial Workflow or Scene-First Prose Judgment.

If the issue is chapter-level flow, hand off to Chapter Assembly & Split Check.

If the issue is canon geography, custom, social structure, or history, hand off to Canon Guard.

If living world repair makes the scene synthetic, hand off to Anti-AI Composite Check.
