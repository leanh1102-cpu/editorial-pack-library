# SLTD Author Voice Fingerprint Calibration

These samples calibrate author-voice and ghostwriter discipline for SLTD.

They are not canon and are not source text to imitate blindly.

## 1. Sample coverage must be explicit

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The agent says it knows the author's voice from chat history.

WHY BAD:
- chat memory is not an approved author sample
- long-form consistency needs source-backed evidence

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
AUTHOR SAMPLE STATUS: missing or partial
SAFE CLAIM: can describe user-stated preferences, not full fingerprint
NEXT NODE: request/provide approved sample or read AUTHOR_TASTE_EXAMPLES.md
```

## 2. Learn scene thinking, not catchphrases

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The style guide lists favorite phrases and repeats them.

WHY BAD:
- repeated phrases make prose formulaic
- author voice is a method of pressure, withholding, and rhythm, not a sticker set

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
NARRATION RHYTHM: evidence-backed
OBJECT / BODY HABITS: evidence-backed
MYSTERY HANDLING: evidence-backed
DO NOT: repeat phrases as signature
```

## 3. Author voice must not overwrite character voice

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Thúy Hoa, Đại Sơn, Đại Phong, and Lão Hoắc all speak in the same polished author rhythm.

WHY BAD:
- character voice and relationship pressure disappear
- author voice becomes a flattening force

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
AUTHOR RHYTHM: narration layer only where appropriate
CHARACTER VOICE: obey relationship, age, household pressure, and xưng hô
VERDICT: author match fails if dialogue collapses
```

## 4. Ghostwriter is bounded by source

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The ghostwriter invents an object, route, or scene beat because it sounds like SLTD.

WHY BAD:
- source-safe prose matters more than style fluency
- good style can hide canon drift

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
DRAFT PERMISSION: blocked until Scene Question, Must Show, Must Not Reveal, Reader Effect, POV, and source surface/packet are clear
```

## 5. Voice match is not a percentage

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The agent reports 95% author style match.

WHY BAD:
- a single score hides canon, POV, character voice, and scene pressure failures

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
AUTHOR RHYTHM MATCH: partial
VIETNAMESE MOUTH-FEEL: pass
OBJECT / BODY PRESSURE: pass
DIALOGUE DIFFERENCE: fail
CANON SAFETY: pass
VERDICT: not ready until dialogue difference is repaired
```

## 6. Human Surface Polish remains downstream

```text
BAD PATTERN:
After ghostwriter drafting, the agent sends prose straight to copyedit/readiness.

WHY BAD:
- author-aligned draft can still be falsely smooth or synthetic

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
NEXT NODE: Human Surface Polish before Copyedit if synthetic-surface risk remains
READINESS: not authorized by ghostwriter pass
```

## 7. C030-style calibration

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The ghostwriter makes SC-030-01 sound author-like by adding elegant dread and polished mystery.

WHY BAD:
- the scene needs one bad morning in a poor kitchen
- author voice must keep survival pressure, not beautify the cold stone

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
SCENE THINKING: survival urge first, lore law second, explanation last
OBJECTS: kệ bếp, chén thuốc, kim vá, áo cũ, chó im, chân kệ lạnh
DO NOT: symbol-first stone, thesis ending, high-lore hint, same-voice family dialogue
NEXT NODE: Source Surface -> Author-Aligned Draft/Rewrite -> Human Surface Polish
```
