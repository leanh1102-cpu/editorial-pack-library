# SLTD Protagonist Inquiry Calibration

These samples calibrate Dai Phong's private reasoning, clue chain, small tests, and supernatural pressure.

They are not canon, not current manuscript, and not prose to imitate.

## 1. Mood-only supernatural sign

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The room becomes cold. Dai Phong feels that an ancient secret is near.

WHY BAD:
- supernatural mood with no question
- adult abstract signal
- no test or return path

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
PRESSURE SIGN: medicine cools too fast
BROKEN DAILY PATTERN: steam does not rise like usual
PRIVATE QUESTION: why did only this bowl cool?
SMALL TEST: he checks the stove edge or waits to see if the dog reacts
PARTIAL READ: the cold does not behave like weather
KEPT DETAIL: he does not tell all of it because the house is already tense
NEXT RETURN: later he checks cold, name, and paper together
```

## 2. Inner reasoning is concrete comparison

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Dai Phong understood that the order of the world was hidden behind ordinary things.

WHY BAD:
- Notion label becomes inner thought
- too adult
- no child-scale reasoning

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
He compares three things: the dog did not bark, Lao Hoac avoided one word, and the old paper made adults lower their voice.
He does not know what it means.
He only marks that all three changed after the object entered the house.
```

## 3. Curiosity must risk something

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Dai Phong asks the right question and receives a useful answer.

WHY BAD:
- clean information handoff
- no cost
- protagonist becomes outline tool

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
PRIVATE QUESTION: why did the name sound wrong?
SMALL TEST: he repeats the name slightly differently or asks about the wrong person
COST: an adult notices he noticed
MISREAD: he thinks the danger is the name itself, not the witness around the name
NEXT RETURN: later he learns a name needs mouth, paper, and witness to stand
```

## 4. Small secret, not major reveal

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The clue reveals the true source of the stone.

WHY BAD:
- reveal too large
- breaks knowledge limit

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
SMALL SECRET: the stone changes how people speak near it
KNOWLEDGE LIMIT: no source, no system, no full rule
AFTERSHOCK: Dai Phong starts checking who changes speech around objects, paper, and names
```

## 5. Monologue should not replace action

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Dai Phong thinks for a long paragraph and decides the event is suspicious.

WHY BAD:
- thought without action
- slow and explanatory

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
He thinks one short comparison, then does one small action: moves a bowl, touches ash, waits for the dog, asks sideways, or keeps silent.
The action carries the thought.
```
