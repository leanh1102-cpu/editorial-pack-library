# SLTD Vietnamese Senior Editor Surface Calibration

These samples calibrate senior Vietnamese surface reading, semantic load, and pass integrity.

They are not canon, not current manuscript, and not prose to imitate.

## 1. Pass metadata is not prose readiness

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Anti-AI Chapter Pass: Pass.
Candidate-ready for Human Chapter Pass.
The agent says the chapter is ready.

WHY BAD:
- anti-AI precheck is not human prose pass
- candidate-ready is not ready
- Publication Lock may still be Not Ready

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
PASS METADATA: Anti-AI Pass yes; Human Chapter Pass no; Publication Lock not ready
STATUS VERDICT: VIETNAMESE_SURFACE_PARTIAL / HUMAN_READER_PASS_REQUIRED
SAFE REPAIR: read exact prose excerpt before readiness
```

## 2. Object density can expose the schema

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Every sentence carries fog, mud, string, mark, knife, stone, name, and witness pressure.

WHY BAD:
- all objects are valid but too visibly functional
- the paragraph reads like a gate map

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
Keep one pressure object dominant.
Let one object stay ordinary.
Move one meaning into body reaction or silence.
Add a small human breath between pressure beats.
```

## 3. Vietnamese read-aloud friction

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The syntax is grammatically correct but stiff, clipped, or translated-feeling.

WHY BAD:
- sentence passes information but not mouth rhythm
- Vietnamese reader hears arrangement before scene

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
Read aloud.
Mark where breath catches for the wrong reason.
Prefer a natural Vietnamese clause over a compressed diagnostic phrase.
```

## 4. Dialogue must have mouth, age, relation, and work

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Characters speak only to deliver scene function.

WHY BAD:
- static talk
- same-voice pressure
- dialogue becomes outline in costume

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
Check who has power, who is tired, who is hiding, who is doing work with hands.
Let speech break around action, habit, avoidance, and relation.
```

## 5. Negative explanation stack

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The prose repeatedly says what something is not, what the character does not know, or what lore is not revealed.

WHY BAD:
- reveal control becomes visible
- the reader feels the author protecting the outline

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
Replace negative guard with positive scene fact.
Instead of explaining what is not known, show what is touched, heard, avoided, or misnamed.
```

## 6. Human breath space

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Every paragraph advances cost, clue, symbol, thread, and pressure.

WHY BAD:
- webnovel propulsion becomes mechanical
- reader has no ordinary life to trust

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
Allow a small action to be mostly ordinary: tying a knot, wiping a bowl, waiting for water, shifting a foot.
Pressure may sit behind it, but the sentence should not announce the function.
```
