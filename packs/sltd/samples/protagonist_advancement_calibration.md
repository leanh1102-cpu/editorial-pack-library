# SLTD Protagonist Advancement Calibration

These samples calibrate how to read and repair Dai Phong's growth route.

They are not canon, not current manuscript, and not prose to imitate.

Use them to identify gain target, cost, knowledge limit, forbidden gain, and aftershock.

## Sample schema

```text
NODE:
BAD PATTERN:
WHY BAD:
PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
WHY IT WORKS:
DO NOT OVERUSE:
RELATED GATE:
```

## 1. Object is not a function tag

```text
NODE: cold stone in the house
```

### BAD PATTERN

```text
The cold stone represents the law of name, body, price, and debt.
```

### WHY BAD

- Notion label becomes prose.
- Object is used as proof item.
- Dai Phong gains a concept too cleanly.

### PREFERRED CALIBRATION

```text
GAIN TARGET: world-rule sensitivity
VISIBLE OBJECT: cold stone / kitchen shelf / medicine bowl
BODY TRACE: cold changes breath and hand behavior
RELATION TRACE: family changes how they speak and hide
KNOWLEDGE LIMIT: Dai Phong does not know the system; he only learns the object is not passive
AFTERSHOCK: later he reads names, paper, debt, and hidden objects as things that may measure a person
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- Lore becomes behavior and pressure.
- Dai Phong gains a reading method, not a lecture.
- Object remains material first.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not turn every strange object into a world-rule lesson.

### RELATED GATE

Prose Craft / Anti-AI Composite / Timeline POV

## 2. Gain must have cost

```text
NODE: first small resistance around scale / witness / village rule
```

### BAD PATTERN

```text
Dai Phong cleverly exposes the unfair scale and everyone understands.
```

### WHY BAD

- Clean genius win.
- No social cost.
- Too adult reasoning.

### PREFERRED CALIBRATION

```text
GAIN TARGET: social reading + resource logic
VISIBLE OBJECT: scale, medicine packet, tally mark, light, witness
COST: food, safety, attention from a stronger adult
KNOWLEDGE LIMIT: he does not defeat the rule; he only makes the tilt harder to hide
AFTERSHOCK: he learns correctness needs position and witness
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- Growth is small and costly.
- Victory is partial.
- Method can return later with paper, route, tax, and pass.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not make every chapter a clever social trap.

### RELATED GATE

Narrative Beat / Character Agency / Timeline POV

## 3. Lore is not protagonist insight by itself

```text
NODE: old paper / old case / village memory
```

### BAD PATTERN

```text
Dai Phong realizes the truth of the old world rule.
```

### WHY BAD

- System knowledge too early.
- Adult abstraction.
- No misread.

### PREFERRED CALIBRATION

```text
GAIN TARGET: language-record sensitivity
VISIBLE OBJECT: old paper, broken name, household example
WHAT HE CAN LEARN: a record can make a person safer or more exposed
WHAT HE MISREADS: he thinks the paper itself protects; later he learns the signer/witness matters
FORBIDDEN GAIN: no high-lore explanation, no full metaphysics
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- Knowledge remains child-scale and source-safe.
- Misread creates future growth.
- Paper becomes route pressure, not exposition.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not make every document ominous.

### RELATED GATE

Timeline POV / Prose Craft / Character Distinctiveness

## 4. Relation debt must move the gain

```text
NODE: Thuy Hoa's hand / Dai Son's breath
```

### BAD PATTERN

```text
Dai Phong understands his parents' sacrifice and becomes more determined.
```

### WHY BAD

- Emotion named.
- Generic motivation.
- No scene mechanism.

### PREFERRED CALIBRATION

```text
GAIN TARGET: moral-Dao pressure
VISIBLE OBJECT: medicine bowl, sleeve, needle, bedding
BODY TRACE: mother's hand stops / father's breath shortens
RELATION TRACE: the family changes a decision because Dai Phong noticed too much or said too little
COST: parental worry or household safety increases
AFTERSHOCK: Dai Phong learns that knowing can drag others into cost
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- Relation debt is embodied.
- Growth hurts someone nearby.
- Motivation is not declared.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not turn every parental gesture into solemn sacrifice.

### RELATED GATE

Character Distinctiveness / Narrative Beat / Prose Craft

## 5. Block forbidden gain

```text
NODE: any early Hồn sương / hidden-system contact
```

### BAD PATTERN

```text
Dai Phong senses the ancient mechanism and forms a clear inner theory.
```

### WHY BAD

- Knowledge limit breaks.
- Hidden lore revealed early.
- Protagonist becomes adult-system narrator.

### PREFERRED CALIBRATION

```text
GAIN TARGET: body + perception
WHAT HE CAN LEARN: the body reacts before thought; some cold/silence is wrong
WHAT HE MUST NOT KNOW: source, system, full metaphysics, named power path
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY: he withholds a small detail because saying it would make the house unsafe
AFTERSHOCK: later he trusts body traces as warnings, but may misread them
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- Early contact gives method, not mastery.
- Misread remains possible.
- Future power route stays earned.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not make every body signal accurate.

### RELATED GATE

Timeline POV / Character Agency / Source Fidelity
