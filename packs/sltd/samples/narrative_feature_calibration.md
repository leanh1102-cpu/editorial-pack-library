# SLTD Narrative Feature Calibration

These samples calibrate narrative-feature and structural-idiosyncrasy review for SLTD.

They are not canon and are not prose to imitate.

## 1. Surface-human prose can still fail structurally

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The scene has hands, breath, smell, weather, and smooth Vietnamese, but the story resolves because the protagonist understands what happened.

WHY BAD:
- bodily detail performs human prose without forcing cost
- protagonist understanding replaces action, debt, witness, or loss

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
BODY / SENSORY OVERPERFORMANCE: present
PROTAGONIST AGENCY SHAPE: under-costed understanding resolution
SAFE REPAIR: make the existing object/body pressure alter a choice, relation, access, or debt
NEXT NODE: Structural / Tracking / Rewrite, not Human Surface Polish
```

## 2. Theme spoken too cleanly

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Narration explains what the village, debt, name, or law means before a scene makes the reader feel the cost.

WHY BAD:
- theme replaces event
- reader is told what to think

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
THEMATIC EXPLICITNESS: high
AI-STRUCTURAL SMELL: theme overexplained
SAFE REPAIR: move theme into object, procedure, witness silence, payment, access block, or dialogue avoidance
```

## 3. Causality too tidy

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Each beat follows the previous beat with clean explanation and no leftover uncertainty.

WHY BAD:
- mystery loses reread value
- the scene feels designed instead of lived

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
CAUSAL TIDINESS: high
REVEAL / RECONTEXTUALIZATION: weak
SAFE REPAIR: keep concrete cause/effect but leave one source-safe unanswered pressure behind
```

## 4. Single-track plot risk

```text
BAD PATTERN:
A chapter does one problem, one clue, one reaction, one solution, one clean next step.

WHY BAD:
- webnovel momentum thins
- long-form continuity has no cross-pressure

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
SINGLE-TRACK PLOT RISK: high
SUBPLOT / THREAD INTEGRATION: thin
SAFE REPAIR: use existing thread/debt/residue/witness only; do not invent a new subplot
```

## 5. Body detail must become consequence

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Cold, skin, breath, trembling, and silence repeat, but nothing changes because of them.

WHY BAD:
- sensory detail becomes decoration
- SLTD taste is imitated at surface only

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
BODY / SENSORY OVERPERFORMANCE: present
CHANGED-STATE / REREAD VALUE: low
SAFE REPAIR: make one body/object detail force action, concealment, misrecognition, delay, injury, debt, or witness risk
```

## 6. Setting as psychological mirror

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Rain, darkness, smoke, or cold mirrors the character's feeling too neatly.

WHY BAD:
- setting becomes mood label
- object/world pressure stops acting independently

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
SETTING AS PSYCHOLOGICAL MIRROR: high
SAFE REPAIR: let the setting interfere with work, speech, travel, medicine, witness, or access
```

## 7. C030-style calibration

```text
BAD PATTERN:
SC-030-01 uses the cold stone to state fate, fear, or family doom in elegant prose.

WHY BAD:
- source pressure becomes theme
- the kitchen becomes symbolic stage instead of poor household worksite
- the stone is understood too soon

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
SCENE QUESTION: Is the cold object dangerous inside Đại Sơn's house now?
THEMATIC EXPLICITNESS: avoid direct theme
BODY / SENSORY OVERPERFORMANCE: pass only if cold changes medicine, hand, dog silence, name-call, or family choice
REVEAL / RECONTEXTUALIZATION: keep source hidden, let later scene reframe this one
NEXT NODE: Source Surface -> Narrative Feature Audit -> Rewrite/Trim -> Human Surface Polish
```

## 8. Readiness is not authorized

```text
BAD PATTERN:
A scene passes Narrative Feature Audit and is called ready.

WHY BAD:
- narrative-feature pass is not Human Chapter Pass
- publication readiness requires current source, chapter assembly, human surface, copy/proof, and explicit lock evidence

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
OUTPUT: NARRATIVE_FEATURE_READY only for this gate
NEXT NODE: downstream prose/readiness route as required
```
