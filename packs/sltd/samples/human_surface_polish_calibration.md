# SLTD Human Surface Polish Calibration

These samples calibrate human-surface polish for SLTD.

They are not canon, not source, and not prose to imitate blindly.

## 1. Do not humanize by smoothing

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The editor makes a poor kitchen scene elegant, balanced, and symbolic.

WHY BAD:
- survival roughness is removed
- the object becomes a motif instead of a household danger
- poverty turns decorative

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
FALSE SMOOTHING RISK: high
OBJECT / BODY LOSS: medicine heat, needle, hand, dog silence weakened
SAFE PATCH: restore the object that changes action before improving rhythm
NEXT NODE: Human Surface Polish only after source surface is read
```

## 2. Same meaning is not enough

```text
BAD PATTERN:
A sentence keeps the same plot fact but removes hesitation, class pressure, and relationship history.

WHY BAD:
- fiction meaning includes who can say it, how they say it, and what they avoid saying
- same semantic content can still violate voice and relationship memory

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
VOICE FLATTENING RISK: present
RELATIONSHIP LOSS: present
SAFE PATCH: keep rough spoken Vietnamese where character pressure requires it
```

## 3. Do not polish hidden canon into POV

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The prose hints that Đại Phong senses the true rank or future role of an object before the scene permits it.

WHY BAD:
- hidden canon leaks into early POV
- mystery becomes explanation
- future payoff loses pressure

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
POV KNOWS: touch, cold, weight, witness behavior, wrong sound, household risk
POV DOES NOT KNOW: origin, rank, high law, future function
PROSE PERMISSION: sensory pressure only
```

## 4. Human surface keeps useful roughness

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The editor removes repeated short lines because they look unsophisticated.

WHY BAD:
- fear, fatigue, shame, or household argument may need uneven rhythm
- perfect flow can make danger feel safe

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
READ-ALOUD RESULT: rough but intentional
SAFE PATCH: cut only accidental repetition; keep pressure repetition when it changes body or decision
```

## 5. Human polish is not line surgery

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The pass rewrites half the excerpt and changes sequence, outcome, and reveal timing.

WHY BAD:
- repair load exceeds polish scope
- the editor is solving structure through surface

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
PROSE PERMISSION: blocked for polish
NEXT NODE: Line Surgery or Rewrite Scene
```

## 6. Detector-bypass framing is blocked

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The task asks to make prose bypass AI detectors.

WHY BAD:
- detector evasion is not an editorial standard
- it encourages surface tricks over story truth

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
AI-SYNTHETIC SMELL: identify concrete prose problems
SAFE PATCH: improve Vietnamese mouth-read, voice difference, object/body pressure, and source fidelity
OUTPUT: DETECTOR_BYPASS_FRAMING if the task is framed as evasion
```

## 7. C030-style calibration

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The cold stone is described as an elegant symbol of fate.

WHY BAD:
- C030 requires survival urge first, lore law second, explanation last
- the stone is first a household danger, not a beautiful mystery object

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
OBJECTS IN PLAY: stove shelf, medicine bowl, needle, cloth, dog silence, hidden wrapped object
FALSE SMOOTHING RISK: high
SAFE PATCH: let cold affect medicine, hand, call, dog, and family choice before any abstract line
NEXT NODE: Source Surface + Human Surface Polish only after exact prose is read
```
