# SLTD Supernatural Event Residue Calibration

These samples calibrate dread amplitude, event residue, folklore incident, beast residue, and relic trace.

They are not canon, not current manuscript, and not prose to imitate.

## 1. Fog is not only atmosphere

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The fog is cold and quiet.

WHY BAD:
- mood without event residue
- no body, animal, route, or social cost

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
RESIDUE TYPE: ecological dread
EVENT ROOT: people have learned that wrong fog eats sound and hides animal movement
PRESENT SIGN: dogs stop answering across the lane
BODY RESIDUE: breath shortens, old wound aches, child cough changes
SOCIAL RESIDUE: adults stop calling names from inside the house
DREAD AMPLITUDE: medium
REVEAL BOUNDARY: no explanation of Hồn sương mechanism
```

## 2. Beast rumor must have a consequence

```text
BAD PATTERN:
Old people say a beast lives in the forest.

WHY BAD:
- folklore without incident
- beast as generic threat

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
RESIDUE TYPE: beast / folklore incident
EVENT ROOT: one winter hunt returned with hide but no dog
ANIMAL RESIDUE: village dogs refuse a bamboo edge at night
ECONOMIC COST: hunters avoid a route, meat price changes, bait becomes expensive
FOLKLORE TRANSMISSION: old people do not name the beast, only say which sound not to answer
REVEAL BOUNDARY: no species taxonomy yet
```

## 3. Relic trace should disturb life

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The bronze fragment hints at ancient war.

WHY BAD:
- relic as decoration
- deep lore label

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
RESIDUE TYPE: relic trace
EVENT ROOT: locked; likely old mechanism / battlefield residue
PRESENT SIGN: water beside it skins over too soon or a tally mark refuses to darken
MATERIAL RESIDUE: metal smell, green stain, wrong cold, ink behavior
SOCIAL RESIDUE: adult hides it from ordinary counting or refuses to put it near medicine
WHAT DAI PHONG CAN SEE: object makes people change handling
WHAT READER CAN SUSPECT: old thing is not dead
```

## 4. Shrine dread needs social memory

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The shrine feels mysterious.

WHY BAD:
- sacred mood only
- no incident or taboo

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
RESIDUE TYPE: shrine taboo / folklore incident
EVENT ROOT: a previous offering season ended with one household losing its work share or name at the well
SOCIAL RESIDUE: people stand in a changed order, avoid a word, send children behind adults
ANIMAL RESIDUE: chicken, dog, or insects react differently near the threshold
REVEAL BOUNDARY: local taboo visible; deity truth hidden
```

## 5. Ancient war residue is not exposition

```text
BAD PATTERN:
The narration explains that the old war involved the Ninth God.

WHY BAD:
- deep lore revealed too early
- protagonist knowledge leak

PREFERRED CALIBRATION:
RESIDUE TYPE: old battlefield leak
EVENT ROOT: locked ancient war trace
PRESENT SIGN: map distance does not match walking time, old ink does not dry, number mark repeats
SOCIAL RESIDUE: people use a longer road without explaining why
WHAT DAI PHONG CAN SEE: route, ink, number, or body fatigue does not match ordinary logic
WHAT DAI PHONG MISREADS: he blames bad map or old habit first
REVEAL BOUNDARY: no Cửu Thần / Bát Thần / Ninth God explanation
```
