# SLTD Prose Craft, Genre Style & Material Calibration

These samples calibrate craft moves for SLTD.

They are not canon, not current manuscript, and not a voice to imitate.

Use them to learn condition, degree, and repair principle.

## Sample schema

```text
CRAFT MOVE:
USE WHEN:
BAD:
WHY BAD:
PREFERRED:
WHY IT WORKS:
DO NOT OVERUSE:
RELATED GATE:
```

## 1. Return to ground after dialogue

```text
CRAFT MOVE: return to ground after dialogue
USE WHEN: dialogue starts to carry explanation without room, body, or object pressure
```

### BAD

```text
“Chuyện này rất nguy hiểm,” nàng nói, vẻ mặt đầy lo lắng.
```

### WHY BAD

- dialogue explains danger;
- emotion is named;
- no object or body keeps pressure alive.

### PREFERRED

```text
“Cất đi.”

Thúy Hoa nói xong mới nhận ra tay mình vẫn đặt trên miệng chén thuốc.
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- warning becomes practical;
- object carries pressure;
- body exposes fear without naming it.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not attach a hand, bowl, door, or breath to every line of dialogue.

### RELATED GATE

Dialogue Voice / Vietnamese Line Surgery / Character Distinctiveness

## 2. Enter through task, not meaning

```text
CRAFT MOVE: enter through task
USE WHEN: a scene starts with abstract mood, fate, omen, or theme
```

### BAD

```text
Một cảm giác bất an bao trùm căn nhà, như thể vận mệnh đã âm thầm đổi hướng.
```

### WHY BAD

- announces meaning before scene action;
- abstract pressure;
- reads like AI literary framing.

### PREFERRED

```text
Tro trong bếp vón lại. Đại Sơn dùng que cời mãi không tơi, còn chén thuốc bên cạnh nguội nhanh hơn mọi ngày.
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- task exists before meaning;
- environment changes action;
- dread comes from household material.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not make every task ominous. Let some tasks stay ordinary.

### RELATED GATE

Living World / Scene-First / Vietnamese Register

## 3. Hold mystery behind concrete pressure

```text
CRAFT MOVE: delayed answer through object pressure
USE WHEN: the prose explains taboo, rule, or mystery too early
```

### BAD

```text
Đây là một cấm kỵ cổ xưa mà người trong thôn không được nhắc đến.
```

### WHY BAD

- explains rule before behavior earns it;
- frontloads lore;
- removes reader pressure.

### PREFERRED

```text
Lão Hoắc nghe đến cái tên ấy thì không hỏi nữa. Ông lấy chén úp lên mặt giấy, mắt vẫn nhìn ra cửa.
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- taboo acts before being explained;
- object and doorway hold pressure;
- the reader feels a rule without receiving a lecture.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not withhold everything. Withholding must create pressure, not confusion.

### RELATED GATE

Vietnamese Register / Narrative Beat / Living World

## 4. Make summary earn compression

```text
CRAFT MOVE: summary compression
USE WHEN: travel, repeated work, or non-pressure movement would drag if dramatized
```

### BAD

```text
Họ đi qua con đường dài, nhìn thấy nhiều nhà cửa và cây cối, rồi cuối cùng đến chợ.
```

### WHY BAD

- movement has no pressure;
- generic travel;
- setting does not change access, cost, rumor, or choice.

### PREFERRED

```text
Đến đầu chợ, bùn trên dép Đại Phong đã khô thành vảy. Ba người nhìn thấy vết ấy trước khi nhìn mặt hắn.
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- compresses travel;
- one residue carries social pressure;
- arrival changes witness and risk.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not turn every transition into a clue.

### RELATED GATE

Scene Composition / Living World / Narrative Beat

## 5. Give off-POV conflict a return path

```text
CRAFT MOVE: off-POV pressure return
USE WHEN: a parallel event exists but may become decoration
```

### BAD

```text
Ở nơi khác, hai người tranh cãi gay gắt về chuyện trong thôn.
```

### WHY BAD

- conflict is abstract;
- no anchor;
- no route back to main pressure.

### PREFERRED

```text
Ở quán nước đầu thôn, người bán trầu gạt chén sang một bên. “Từ sáng đến giờ, nhà ấy không nhóm bếp.”
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- outside scene returns through rumor;
- witness and household routine become pressure;
- conflict can affect access, suspicion, or timing later.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not cut away only to make the world feel large.

### RELATED GATE

Narrative Beat / Living World

## 6. Character voice through relation, not accent costume

```text
CRAFT MOVE: relation-bound voice
USE WHEN: characters sound interchangeable
```

### BAD

```text
“Chúng ta cần suy xét kỹ lưỡng trước khi đưa ra quyết định.”
```

### WHY BAD

- polished logic;
- no age, work, fear, or relation;
- could belong to anyone.

### PREFERRED

```text
“Đừng vội.” Lão Hoắc nhìn Đại Phong, rồi nhìn sang Thúy Hoa. “Trong nhà còn trẻ con.”
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- voice is restrained;
- relation changes who he looks at;
- fear appears through social consequence.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not give every character a catchphrase or fixed gesture.

### RELATED GATE

Character Distinctiveness / Dialogue Voice

## 7. Scene-native image instead of literary decoration

```text
CRAFT MOVE: scene-native image
USE WHEN: prose uses attractive image unrelated to material in scene
```

### BAD

```text
Nỗi sợ len như sương mỏng qua những vết nứt trong lòng nàng.
```

### WHY BAD

- pretty but generic;
- emotion is abstract;
- image does not arise from action.

### PREFERRED

```text
Nàng bưng chén thuốc lên rồi đặt xuống. Mặt nước sóng sánh, va vào thành chén một tiếng rất khẽ.
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- image comes from object;
- sound changes reader breath;
- fear remains embodied.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not turn every object into a metaphor.

### RELATED GATE

Vietnamese Line Surgery / Scene Composition

## 8. Aftershock as residue

```text
CRAFT MOVE: residue aftershock
USE WHEN: a turn needs inherited pressure after the scene ends
```

### BAD

```text
Từ khoảnh khắc đó, mọi thứ đã thay đổi mãi mãi.
```

### WHY BAD

- declares changed state;
- no residue;
- no practical consequence.

### PREFERRED

```text
Ngoài hiên, con chó vàng thôi cào cửa.
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- small physical trace;
- altered animal behavior signals pressure;
- aftershock stays concrete.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not end every scene with a cold one-line punch.

### RELATED GATE

Narrative Beat / Webnovel Paragraphing / Dynamic Range

## 9. Controlled Viet Dao register

```text
CRAFT MOVE: one earned formal term
USE WHEN: old-world pressure needs weight but prose risks Hán Việt overload
```

### BAD

```text
Khí tức cổ xưa từ cổ vật khiến tâm thần mọi người chấn động.
```

### WHY BAD

- abstract Sino-Vietnamese cluster;
- no body, object handling, or cost;
- translated xianxia smell.

### PREFERRED

```text
Lão Hoắc chỉ vào nét triện bị mốc ăn mất một nửa. “Đừng đọc thành tiếng.”
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- one precise formal term;
- practical warning;
- object pressure first.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not replace every strange thing with a formal term.

### RELATED GATE

Vietnamese Register

## 10. Choose repair type before patching

```text
CRAFT MOVE: repair decision
USE WHEN: a passage has multiple flaws and the agent wants to patch everything
```

### BAD

```text
Add more body detail, more silence, more object pressure, more world texture, and more voice difference all at once.
```

### WHY BAD

- craft stacking;
- hides structural weakness;
- creates polished AI collage.

### PREFERRED

```text
Earliest blocker: dialogue is explaining the scene.
Selected move: return to ground after dialogue.
Patch only that layer first.
```

### WHY IT WORKS

- one move matches one blocker;
- reduces checklist prose;
- preserves source and scene function.

### DO NOT OVERUSE

Do not keep patching when the passage needs a scene rewrite.

### RELATED GATE

First-Pass / Anti-AI Composite / Scene-First
