# Foundation Curriculum

**Status:** `FOUNDATION-INTEGRATED v0.5 / VALIDATED CANDIDATE LAYER`

Foundation teaches eight linked distinctions:

1. `F1 Language as Evidence` — observable wording/context before inference; sentence meaning vs situated utterance meaning.
2. `F2 Sentence Mechanics` — redundancy, mediation, marked order, ellipsis, focus, reference; context before normalization.
3. `F3 Rhythm and Punctuation as Function` — pause/fracture/repetition/length against local function; punctuation is not identical to exact spoken intonation.
4. `F4 Voice, Speech Condition, and Register` — speaker/addressee/relation/role/setting/purpose; preserve dialect and stateful register shifts.
5. `F5 Meaning, Implication, and Reader Inference` — explicit statement vs implicature vs reader/editor inference; fair inference without mind-reading.
6. `F6 Paragraph Movement` — topic, logical relation, information development, reference, temporal/causal order; connectors are resources, not quotas.
7. `F7 Image and Concrete Grounding` — image/function before sensory inflation; direct grounding only when a demonstrated mechanism needs it.
8. `F8 Close Reading → Craft Transfer` — `chữ → cú pháp → nhịp → hình thức → điểm nhìn/giọng → cấu trúc → hiệu ứng → diễn giải → craft transfer`.

## Teaching loop
`DIAGNOSE LEARNER STATE → ONE CONCEPT → TEXTUAL EXAMPLE → GUIDED QUESTION → SMALL DRILL → TRANSFER → RETRIEVAL CHECK`.

One turn should normally teach one mechanism or one contrast. Do not dump all eight modules.

## Evidence discipline
- Observation before interpretation.
- Interpretation retains confidence and alternative readings where relevant.
- Historical/form/cultural claims remain source-gated.
- No module grants permission to normalize intentional voice, rhythm, ambiguity, metaphor, or ellipsis by default.

## Scope lock
Foundation is a validated operational learning layer, not a complete theory of Vietnamese grammar/literature. Broad grammar, historical linguistics, poetics, rhetoric, and form-law expansion remain source-gated beyond the validated narrow claims.
