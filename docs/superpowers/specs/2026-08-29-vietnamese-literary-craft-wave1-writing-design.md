# Vietnamese Literary Craft — Knowledge Expansion Wave 1 (Writing-First) Design

## Goal

Expand the standalone `vietnamese-literary-craft/` package from validated behavioral/curriculum infrastructure into a first source-backed specialized knowledge layer that materially improves prose analysis, composition, and revision without turning heuristics into universal style law.

Wave 1 is writing-first. It does **not** attempt full classical-form coverage, historical linguistics, or corpus completion.

## Change classification

`MINOR` — additive specialized knowledge expansion inside the standalone general skill.

The change must not modify `core/`, `packs/sltd/`, SLTD canon/taste, or project routing. `CONSTITUTION.md` remains immutable. `SKILL.md` stays router-sized; it may receive only narrowly necessary references after the modules validate.

## Current authority

The package on `main` is `PACKAGE-COMPLETE / STATIC-VALIDATED` for the v1 behavioral/curriculum layer. Foundation, Intermediate, Advanced, and Writer Self Review are already validated. Wave 1 must extend those distinctions rather than duplicate or supersede them.

Existing locks remain binding:

- evidence before conclusion;
- function before correction;
- observation before interpretation;
- preserve intentional voice, register, ambiguity, rhythm, reader inference, and controlled deviation;
- unverified information remains claim, not fact;
- smallest defensible intervention;
- no polish for polish's sake;
- self-revision hard cap 5, pass 6 forbidden;
- historical/form claims remain source-gated;
- `SENSORY-MEDIATION v0.1` remains narrow/contact-bound;
- Advanced A6 broad genre taxonomy remains gated;
- WSR stop/release behavior remains internal operational policy.

## Wave 1 module set

Wave 1 contains exactly ten candidate modules.

### KX1 — `analysis/close-reading.md`
Operationalizes `chữ → cú pháp → nhịp → hình thức → điểm nhìn/giọng → cấu trúc → hiệu ứng → diễn giải → craft transfer`. Distinguish observation, inference, interpretation, and transfer. Do not force every reading through every lens.

### KX2 — `theory/semantics-pragmatics/implicature-reference-inference.md`
Narrow scope: sentence meaning vs situated meaning; explicit statement vs implication/inference; reference resolution; speaker/character claim vs narrator fact; context-sensitive interpretation. Not a comprehensive Vietnamese semantics/pragmatics textbook.

### KX3 — `theory/stylistics/functional-deviation.md`
Markedness, deviation, repetition, ellipsis, roughness, register shift, and stylistic choice as function-dependent phenomena. Distinguish controlled deviation from accidental failure and retain counterexamples/leave-alone cases.

### KX4 — `craft/sentence.md`
Reference, information order, clause relation, compression/expansion, rhythm, ellipsis, repetition, mediation, and sentence-bound repair. Do not equate shortness, grammatical regularity, or sensory density with quality.

### KX5 — `craft/paragraph.md`
Topic/focus, reference chains, temporal/causal/logical progression, information development, paragraph breaks, local cohesion, and pressure-shaped pacing. Connectors remain resources, not quotas.

### KX6 — `craft/dialogue.md`
Dialogue as social action: speaker goal, addressee, relation, status, pressure, implication, turn function, withheld information, silence, and stateful register. Protect idiolect/dialect and reject same-voice normalization.

### KX7 — `craft/point-of-view.md`
Narrator vs focalizer/access frame, knowledge boundaries, perceptual/cognitive access, viewpoint shifts, local leakage, strategic withholding, and evidentiary consequences. Do not label every contradiction as unreliable narration or every shift as an error.

### KX8 — `craft/revision.md`
Connect specialized diagnosis to Writer Self Review: `evidence → issue → mechanism → desired effect → smallest useful intervention → regression check → stop`. Do not create a second revision workflow or override cap-5/stop policy.

### KX9 — `register/modern-vietnamese.md`
Working register model for contemporary Vietnamese prose: colloquial/written orientation, social relation, role, setting, purpose, formality, regional/idiolect variation, and literary manipulation. Do not define one prestige variety as universal literary norm.

### KX10 — `register/pseudo-archaic-detection.md`
Conservative diagnostic module for historical/archaizing prose. It may flag unsupported mixtures, generic Hán–Việt inflation, modern discourse markers inside an unmarked period frame, or anachronistic register signals, but must not certify historical correctness from model memory. Missing period/institution/source/edition/gloss policy → `HOLD-INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## Common module contract

Each module exposes:
`CONCEPT, DEFINITION, TERMS, SCOPE, MECHANISM, TEXTUAL SIGNALS, EFFECTS, CONDITIONS, COUNTEREXAMPLES, COMMON ERRORS, HISTORICAL VARIATION, FORM VARIATION, CLOSE READING METHOD, CRAFT TRANSFER, DRILLS, AI OPERATIONAL RULES, SELF-CHECK, SOURCES`.

Unsupported sections state their boundary instead of being filled from model memory.

### Source-debt policy
- No module becomes `STABLE` from abstracts alone.
- Rule-level claims require full-text or sufficiently complete authoritative recovery plus claim extraction.
- Case-study findings remain case-study scoped unless independently supported.
- `pseudo-archaic-detection.md` is diagnostic, not a period-authentication engine.
- Historical lexical claims require dated textual evidence, dictionary/textual scholarship, or equivalent authoritative basis.
- Internal policies such as cap 5/action taxonomy/promotion behavior must not be falsely attributed to external scholarship.

## RED harness before module authoring

Create `KNOWLEDGE-WAVE1-RED-24 v0.1` before injecting Wave 1 module rules.

Required pressure families:
1. observation vs interpretation;
2. implicature vs explicit fact;
3. ambiguous reference with material consequence;
4. intentional fragment/ellipsis vs normalization;
5. functional repetition vs accidental duplication;
6. sentence length without demonstrated failure;
7. paragraph cohesion vs connector inflation;
8. dialogue social action vs same-voice smoothing;
9. prepared/formal speech vs baseline voice;
10. POV access leakage vs legitimate viewpoint shift;
11. strategic withholding vs missing fair-play evidence;
12. local repair vs global rewrite;
13. revision regression after a cleaner edit;
14. modern register variation without prestige normalization;
15. regional/idiolect preservation;
16. pseudo-archaic Hán–Việt inflation;
17. historical term without source evidence → HOLD;
18. explicitly modernized historical document → preserve;
19. stylistic roughness with functional pressure;
20. metaphor/marked syntax leave-alone case;
21. close-reading alternative interpretation;
22. craft transfer mechanism without surface imitation;
23. sensory grounding without universalization;
24. stop when no material failure remains.

Scoring `2/1/0`, maximum `48`.

Hard fails: unsupported fact promotion; prestige normalization of dialect; historical/register verdict without evidence; flattening functional deviation for smoothness; second revision workflow/pass-5 violation; surface imitation in transfer; frequency/count promoted into literary law.

A strong RED baseline means formalize distinctions and sources; do not invent corrective rules merely because the suite is easy.

## Claim extraction and authoring sequence

1. Run RED baseline.
2. Acquire/read authoritative sources for exact claims needed.
3. Create claim ledger: `SOURCE / READ_LEVEL / SUPPORTED CLAIM / LIMIT / MODULE`.
4. Author KX1–KX10 as `SOURCE-BACKED CANDIDATE` modules.
5. Blind bundled validation with at least two pressure cases per module plus cross-layer sentinels.
6. Promote only modules that pass without damaging existing preservation/epistemic/register behavior.
7. Run compact Wave 1 integration against Foundation/Intermediate/Advanced/WSR.
8. Update `SKILL.md` routing references only after integration and only if materially necessary.

## Validation gates

RED is diagnostic only.

Module validation target: all required cases `2/2`, hard fail 0, no preservation/epistemic/register regressions. Narrow evidence may produce `CANDIDATE-STABLE — NARROW SOURCE SCOPE`.

Promotion to `KNOWLEDGE-WAVE1-INTEGRATED v0.1` requires full integration score, hard fail 0, no relevant BASELINE-36/sensory/cross-layer regression, preserved source boundaries, and no rule derived solely from frequency or taste.

## Regression radius

Any Wave 1 change must at minimum rerun preservation, epistemic status, voice/register, POV/access, historical HOLD, sensory-mediation narrowness, revision scope and stop-condition sentinels. Changes to `SKILL.md`, shared routing, or shared theory require broader regression.

## Rollback

Wave 1 is additive. Rollback removes/reverts Wave 1 module/harness files and any narrow router reference added after validation. Existing curriculum and SLTD packs remain unchanged.

## Non-goals

No full Vietnamese grammar; no universal style prescription; no comprehensive classical-form law; no historical authenticity certification from model memory; no full corpus build; no GitHub Actions/boards/issues/project-management artifacts/new Notion database; no SLTD-specific canon/taste; no PAI dependency.
